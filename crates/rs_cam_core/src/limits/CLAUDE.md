# `limits/` — one limit name, one check record

Feeds contract P29-2, P29-3, P29-6 and P29-7 (`planning/feeds_contract_2026-11-03/phases/`). The gates read `CutGroup`, `ByGroup` and `scope` since P29-6 6d; the limits before the simulation read the same `scope` since P29-7 7c (`feeds::planned`).

- `scope.rs` (P29-6 6d, P29-7 7c) — `scope(LimitId, CutGroup)`: `Judged`, `NotJudged` (row id, clause) or `NotACutLimit(PlanReason)` (a closed list: `DepthOnly`, `PlanLevel`, `MachineOrRpm`, `DrillGate`). - `exemptions.rs` (P29-3 A) — `EXEMPTIONS`, the one list of every exemption from a limit. A scope read in product code carries a marker comment `EXEMPT` with its row ids; a row names the files of its markers.

## Invariants

1. No signature and no code of this folder names an operation type, an operation family or a pass role, and the folder imports nothing from `compute/` or `session/`. A limit sees the cut, the tool, the stock and the machine (P29 design 4.5). The sentry scans the folder. P29-last (L-5): the guard `tests/no_limit_reads_the_op_type_p29_last.rs` and the types (`CutFacts` and `feeds::RowKey` with private fields; `limits::scope`, `planned_limit_of` and `tool_load::groups::gate_of` with no wildcard arm) hold this rule, not this text: a new mechanism (force was one) declares its fact in `cuts()` and judges every cut group, before and after the simulation.
2. Each mapping from an old list is a match that names every variant. A second argument that tells two limits of one old name is a typed value (`ChipSide`), never a bool.
3. `Geometry` is a closed list of geometry reasons. An exemption is `Physical(Geometry)`, `NeedsRuling` or `Ruled` (a quoted operator ruling); no fourth class. A limit that an operation skips by a scope decision is `NoNumber(NoBound)` or `NoNumber(NotJudgedHere)`, never `NotApplicable`. P29-11 11-2, 11-2b: a row of a `limits::scope` cell takes the class of its own row by ONE function, `scope_cell_state`, on both sides (the envelope cell and the gate cell of `rows_of_verdict`): `Physical(g)` gives `NotApplicable(g)` (the drill chip band P28-20, deflection P28-17, force budget P28-04), any other class `NoNumber(NotJudgedHere)` (drill power, P28-10); the row names its row in both cases. P29-11 section 13, ruling 4: the headline cell of a drill op is a scope cell too (`gate_rows`, `scope_cell_row`; three callers); the verdict keeps `NotApplicableForOp`.
4. A projection takes the state of the old record. It does not judge a second time: `of_criterion` reads `LoadState`, `of_envelope` reads the margins of `SafeEnvelope::placing_margins` (the fold of `place`); P29-11 11-3: where the placement does not judge (a fixed axis, a per-point limit) it reads the value check of the envelope, `SafeEnvelope::stated_value_check` (the body depth by the one depth door, the body width, the stored feed against the cutting ceiling; a lower bound proves only `Exceeds`).
5. `Basis::setting` and `Basis::gates_export` (`feeds/sourced.rs`) equal the methods of the `BoundSource` that `basis_of_bound` read; a `Derived` basis over a value that does not gate does not gate (P29-10: the envelope force budget, one tone with the gate).

## How to add a limit

1. Add a `LimitId` member. The compiler stops at three matches with no wildcard: `scope` (each `CutGroup` cell: `Judged`, `NotJudged` with its row and marker, or `NotACutLimit` with its reason), `feeds::planned::planned_limit_of` (its role on the plane: `DepthCeiling(CeilingEdge)`, `ValueCheck(ValueCheck)` or `NotACutLimit(reason)`) and `tool_load::groups::gate_of` (its gates, or none with a named exemption row).
2. Before the simulation: one `impl feeds::planned::PlannedLimit` (`judge` over one planned cut) and, for a new `CeilingEdge` or `ValueCheck` variant, one arm in each envelope match (the compiler asks: the edge builder, the pick edge, the exact solve, the rows). The envelope reads every role through one loop (`envelope::plane_roles`); the driver `judge_declared_cuts` asks `scope` and builds the `ByCut` record. No limit decides a scope or walks the cut list.
3. After the simulation: one `tool_load::MetricEvaluator` (`judge` over one group's samples), a variant of `groups::Gate`, and a field of `tool_load::verdict::GroupVerdicts` (built in `evaluate_population`). The sentry `every_limit_judges_every_declared_cut_p29_7` holds the three lists against each other.
