## Invariants

- One request, two builders (`ProjectSession::run_simulation`, the GUI). Seven SHARED decisions in
  `simulate.rs`: `PhantomPriorStockScan`, `entry_tool_fields`, `group_stock_cut_direction`,
  `entry_metrics_not_applicable`, `group_stock_changes` (S2), `group_obstacles` (K1), `entry_head_fit` (H1). Put an eighth there, not in one builder.
  Both builders carve every enabled result, an empty one too (G-RESTLEVELS: no phantom theft).
- A new operation needs a catalogue row and a config variant that derives `OpParams` (W5: its `#[param]`
  attributes ARE the schema, and the GUI row and the setter read one range). F185: a published number (`ParamKind::is_number`) states a bound, `frame_dependent` or `unbounded = "<reason>"`, or it does not compile; a bound comes only from math, physics or an existing range. W6b: `domain::validate_operation` (each number in its range via `check_param`, the setter's rule; an ordered angle pair `ordered_pair` in order, F036; `Floor::door_refusal`; Profile side On refuses `InControl` (P6.1) and the floor Each picked face (P6.3, `SideOnEachFace`)) runs at AddToolpath, the op change, the setter's generic arm and load. F163: `impl_operation_params!` names all four role clauses (`none` is explicit). F023: code reads `ParamDef::kind`, never a `type_name` prefix. The row's `generate` IS the dispatch; add no second path. The prologue runs first: cancel, the row's `GeometryRequirement`,
  `OperationType::tool_refusal`. An adapter reads `Prepared`; it does not check them again. P2.3a: `GeometryRequirement::{Items, ItemsAndMesh}`; the prologue runs `selection::view(ctx.selection, item_use)` (`SelectionInputs` is `pub(crate)` and its field is private to `prologue.rs`, C3) and gives `Prepared::{regions, curves, holes}`; a refusal is `OperationError::Selection`; ONE `SelectionNoteFinding` holds every note (the open paths, the faces at the top), recorded once in `check_inputs`. Drill and Pin Drill still read `ctx.drill_targets` (P2.3c). No op closes an open path with a chord. P2.3b: `OperationConfig::{selection, selection_slot, default_selection}` (exhaustive); W4 check 9 holds slot = `item_use != None`. P4.2: Face reads `Regions` (default `Selection::stock()`; no `GeometryRequirement::Stock`; `is_stock_based` reads the default selection); only the stock region (`Regions::is_stock`) grows by `stock_offset` (O-3).
- W2.4: a consumer reads `OperationConfig::capabilities()`, never a `match` on the op type. W2.3: a report reaches `GenerationFindings` only through `ctx.record`. W6.2/C3: an adapter reads XY, mask and stock from `Prepared::territory()`; `TerritoryInputs` fields are private to `territory.rs`. Raw stock = `unguarded_stock` (closed list in its doc). W6.3: a Height-field op (Drop Cutter, Scallop, Horizontal, Radial, Spiral, Ramp, V-Carve) writes only `plan`; `run_height_field` owns `refuse`, the query (a handle: each kernel samples at its own resolution), the ONE link stage (`LinkRule::AfterPlan` = `shared::link_after_plan`; Scallop `InPlan` links in its kernel, the iso field reads only the stage ceiling, D1), the report record and the spans (`SpanRule`). W6.4: a Composed op (Unified Finish, Steep and Shallow) writes `classify`, `cut_region`, `route`, `order_constraints`; `run_composed(ctx, prepared, ComposedLink, build)` owns the territory reads (`ComposedInputs`: ceiling, stage, `ContourFilter` rule), the cancel, the record, the spans (barriers only from `order_constraints`), the trace hook and `produced`. W6.5: a Volumetric op (3D Rough) states `OWNS` and `HEIGHTS` (a sentry holds them equal to the row); `run_volumetric` owns the territory, the RAW seed stock, top and floor, the merge channel, cancel, spans, `produced`. A Curve op (Project Curve) writes `plan`, `link`; `run_curve` owns the frame (`ctx.z_flipped()`), the query, the link inputs, `BuriedCurveFinding` (Info), the spans. No session code patches a config copy: pins reach Pin Drill as `ctx.alignment_pins`.
- W3: a Layered op (Pocket, Zigzag, Trace, Profile, Face, Chamfer, Waterline) writes `prepare` + fallible `emit_level` (`LevelRest`, cancel); `Input` = `Polygon2` or a surface (`MeshInput`, W6.1); hooks `outlines`, `ladder`, `after_plan_link`, `spans`, `annotate`, `reach_band`, `off_grid`.
  `run_layered` owns the ladder, rest rule, one prepare per region, `region_ordering` (R4), cancel per level, link, spans. `LevelInfo::final_z`: the Z of the final level (Profile tab top, P5b). Floor (D9): Pocket, Profile, Zigzag, Adaptive 2D hold ONE `floor: Floor` (driver-set; default `Floor::Top { depth }`; `floor.rs`: `Floor`, `FloorRef`, `FloorRefusal`). `Floor::domain` is the one domain of its number: the door `door_refusal` and the schema `floor_schema_json` read it; `OperationConfig::set_floor` is the writer. `HeightContext::floor` makes `ResolvedHeights::bottom_z` the floor; `cutting_levels(&ResolvedHeights)` steps `ops::depth::floor_ladder(top, bottom)`. `Capabilities::floor_owner` (`HeightsBottom`, `OpFloor`, `OpDial`): a reader `match`es it. L-16: an empty ladder refuses (`effective_levels`); no fallback from `heights.depth()`. Under `Floor::EachFace` the prologue runs THE per-area rule `floor::region_floors` (status = generation): it skips a face at the top with a `NoteCause::AtTheTop` note (`OpItems::skip_regions`) and refuses `FloorAboveTop` only when every face is at the top. It gives `Prepared::region_ladders` (each = `cutting_levels` to that floor); `run_layered` steps each outline down its own ladder (one ladder = the old runner, bit for bit; level-first walks the (Z, repeat) union, no float tolerance; one obstacle clip per run of one ladder, `layered::area_pieces_less_obstacles`). Adaptive 2D steps each inset piece down the ladder of its area in its own loop (L-5). `FloorReferences` is THE L-10 rule of the GUI (a change of reference, a drag).
- W3.4: rest rule = `Capabilities::rest_use`. `LevelClip` clips the input; `LevelSkip` only skips (a clipped contour cuts a new wall; Chamfer band = `w + t`; P6.3: `ReachBand.open` holds the open paths, with no chord, lib `an_open_band_has_no_chord_p6_3`); `ContourFilter` is runner-owned (`ContourFilterRule`: `run_layered` for Waterline with its tally, `run_composed` for both halves of Steep and Shallow, W6.4.6); it keeps the runs that pass material (`R + cell`; an air stretch splits only when the retract saves time, `ops::waterline::bridge_air_stretches`, W6.1); Drill
  `StockColumn` lowers each hole top and R-plane (D8). 2D Adaptive `LevelMask` (own loop): `level_masks` skips a level, seeds the planner from the raw mask. - Session stages after generation, not op shapes (D2): the generic rest analysis (`attach_generic_rest_analysis`, one call in `execute_operation_annotated`) and `clip_and_split_entries` (one call in `JobPhases::run_stages`). - A dressup's parameters live INSIDE its `Option` on `DressupConfig` (CUT-13), with no value field beside its bool; wire: `DressupConfigWire`. - K1: EVERY op keeps the tool out of the setup's obstacles, boundary on or off, no opt-in. ONE `ObstacleSet::resolve` (setup frame, NO part transform: table-fixed; refuses a missing model) and ONE `ObstacleToolView` serve the post-dressup step, `link_ceiling_for`, the territory pre-clip (K3:
  `Territory::resolve`, `Territory::area_outlines`), the simulation and `collision_check` (with the `JoinStart`). An obstacle is solid from its top down. Do not gate any of them on the boundary. H1: the head bands, the shoe against the stock box and the shelf Z floor are entries of the same view. K2: a mesh obstacle reads `geom_cache::cached_silhouette`; the cell is `boundary::SILHOUETTE_CELL_SIZE`.
- G-SIMMEM: the loop holds the cut trace ONCE. The memo asks `admits` BEFORE it clones a snapshot; share the trace by `Arc`, never `clone()` it. A checkpoint keeps its mesh inputs; `build_mesh` builds on demand (M2). Checkpoint k's `mesh_stock` is prior k+1: one `Arc`, same group (M3).
  S0: a Z-axis group starts from the last Z-axis group's final stock. S3: its stock changes then apply, to it and (not lateral) to `global_stock`.
