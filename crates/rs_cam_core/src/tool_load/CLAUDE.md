# `tool_load/` — the cutting-load guardrails

Independent gates over a simulated cut. Entry: `tool_load::evaluate_toolpath`.

## Files

- `mod.rs` — the monitor and the report entry point. `context.rs` — `ToolpathLoadContext` (P29-6 6a: private fields, one constructor `of_operation`; the op fills the kind, the row key, `body_facts` and the commanded lift; the context carries the `CapTable` and lends `sample_context()`). `chipload.rs`, `power.rs`,
  `deflection.rs`, `depth.rs`, `plunge_stress.rs` — the milling gates; `cut_force.rs` — the
  report-only force reading (P17; P29-10 10g: `CutPoint3::over_budget` (strict, no tolerance), `over_budget_words`, `force_plane`); `force_field.rs` (10g, moved from viz) — `ForceField` (the BODY cut's `peak_force_n` on a grid, ruling R1), `ForcePlane` (field, chip, the machine `force_budget_n`, `BudgetRole::Limit` or `NoLimitHere`, the level lines at `CONTOUR_SHARES`, traced once), `contours`; `drill_gates.rs` — chip welding, peck, plunge feed.
  `load_fraction.rs` — the one load `L`.
- `groups.rs` (P29-6 6d) — `populations` (one walk: each removing sample in its `CutGroup`), `MetricEvaluator` (a gate: `LIMITS`, `judge` over one group's samples), `judge_by_group` (THE driver: asks `limits::scope`, builds the `ByGroup<GroupCell>` record; the headline group, `ctx.headline_group()`, is the old one row). P29-7 7c: `Gate` (every gate, `limits()`) and `gate_of(LimitId)` (one match, no wildcard; the hand list `GATE_LIMITS` is gone).
- `locality.rs` (`judged_group`, `is_body_without_spans` for the triage), `boundary.rs`, `verdict.rs` (`GroupVerdicts`; P29-10 PB: `ChipBoundsSource::of_row`, THE class of a matched row, and `advisory_low_side` (`AdvisoryLowSide`); the gate and the planned chip floor read them; P29-10 10e: `ToolLoadReport` and `ToolpathLoadVerdict` serialise only and `groups` is not on the wire: the rows carry every cell), `display.rs`, `distribution.rs` (the cards bin the headline group; `DistributionMetric::text` = `MetricText`, the one writer of a chart title, unit and scale), `metric_guide.rs`. `drill_gates.rs`: a plunge-feed outcome reports the NEARER envelope bound; `two_sided_envelope` and `judges_its_floor` say when that bound is the floor (P29-10 10e2).

## Invariants

- P29-6 6d: every gate judges every removing sample in the group `locality::judged_group` gives, through `groups::judge_by_group`; the headline field is the headline group, `ToolpathLoadVerdict::groups` holds every group, and a group that `Exceeds` joins `criteria()` (Q5: a `Within` group gives no diagnostic). P29-10 10c: a group finding carries `Diagnostic::group` (the triage keys on it, `DedupKey::group`) and `ExceedsEntry::group`; `GroupVerdicts::headline_only_for_tests` exists only under `test` or `test-support`. No gate walks the trace for its population or reads the op kind for a scope (`chip_row_key` is the chip row key only).
- P11b2: the chipload gate quantity is the PEAK chip, `display::peak_chip_per_tooth` (advance / `RadialThinning::lift` at the measured arc, `Engagement::from_sample`; rule `ctx.body_facts().thins_chip`, Finish lift 1; `limits::scope` does not judge a drill cycle), on both sides, against the printed band at every depth (no de-rate). P29-6 6b: the population is every sample of the group that removes material (no feed filter); `chipload::has_chip_number` is the validity test (a chip model, not air class); a thinning sample with no engagement, an air-class sample and an arc of 0 count in `missing_arc_count`. The histogram reads it; `commanded_above_band` reads `CommandedStage::peak_chip_mm` (median lift). The heat map (`advance_per_tooth_per_move`) still paints the advance: open gap.
- The cutting load `L` of a pass (chip area over a full pass, per strip) is
  `load_fraction::chip_load_fraction` only (INTEGRATION_PLAN D5, Q1); it reads
  `mrr_cross_section_mm2`, as power does. Never write a second form.
- P29-1: `sample_power_kw`, `sample_tip_deflection_mm`, `cut_force::sample_peak_force_n` and `display::peak_chip_per_tooth` call `feeds::cut_load::CutLoad`; never call a model function here. K7 (P9): the power gate reads the one engagement, `feeds::engagement::Engagement::from_sample`: the width is the geometric inverse `ae = r (1 - cos psi)` of the measured arc (lead decision D-W), the section is the cutter's own; the arc-equivalent slab `(psi / pi) x 2 r` is retired. P22-0c-6b: a tapered ball's width stops at the body's cutting cap (`EngagedDiameter::of_tool`), the cap Suggest reads. The simulation placement on the envelope (`compute::simulate::envelope_placement`) reads the body group only (`judged_group`, ruling Q4, row `P28-21`) and `sample_power_kw`; P20i: it judges power by `power::gate_sample_power` (the gate's feed, width and rated power), never by the drawn Power edge.
- A gate with no population proved nothing; check the count. S6: a sample's
  material is `effective_material_for_sample`; split populations ONLY in `evaluate_toolpath`.
- B6: power and deflection read `Material::force_line()`. Power is `line ×
  grain_factor` (1.0 on every line); no line is `MaterialUnvalidated`. A
  force-line or grain-factor change needs the slow `--test` sims, not `--lib`.
- F197: a stale trace rewrites a verdict through `ToolpathLoadVerdict::mark_simulation_stale` only; it
  destructures every field. F070: build a gate context with `ProjectSession::load_context_parts(..).ctx()`; a test calls `ToolpathLoadContext::of_operation` with a real op (`OperationConfig::new_default`) and `CapTable::empty()`.
- P11a (L5): the deflection gate reads the PEAK engaged-edge force of the sample (`deflection::sample_engagement`: `Engagement::from_tool_cut` at `axial_engagement_mm` and the arc's width `r (1 - cos psi)`), through `feeds::predict::tip_deflection_from_engagement`; the power gate's mean is the same sum's mean. P17: `cut_force::sample_peak_force_n` is that force; the force card, `ToolpathLoadVerdict::cut_force` (report-only, not a criterion) and the deflection gate read one engagement and one law. The budget line exists only where the body cut has the force budget (`depth_cap_of(body_facts().depth_rule)`, `cut_force::budget_line`, lead ruling 3; P29-5: a slot or a groove under every role).
- P17, P29-7 7d (ruling R5): the deflection `Exceeds` limit is `deflection::exceeds_limit`: `min(leave, EXCEEDS_BOUND_MM)`, the leave being the op's radial stock to leave (`body_facts().leave_after_mm`: `Leave::Mm` or `Leave::None`; the role is not read); `DeflectionBasis` is `StockToLeave` (a leave under the target) or `Target`. The envelope reads the same value (`deflection_limit_um`) on every role; the feed modulator keeps 200 um (row `P28-16`, ruling Q4).
- F082: the deflection-cap compliance is `deflection::tip_compliance_mm_per_n` (Suggest's ceiling and the modulator read it). F080: `UnmodeledReason::operator_text` is the reason's one clause.
- The drill gates model the R-plane-rooted emitted schedule and read cutting
  geometry, not fed distance; a tapered drill uses the envelope diameter.
