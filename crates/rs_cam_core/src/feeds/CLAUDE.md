# `feeds/` — feeds and speeds

The calculator for RPM, feed, plunge rate, DOC and WOC. The validated entry point is `feeds::suggest`. This file is the one map of the feeds flow (F144 deleted `INTEGRATION.md`).

## Files
- `mod.rs`, `tests.rs` — the calculator and its unit tests. `suggest.rs` + `suggest/` — the Suggest model and read doors; children `apply.rs` (write-back
  funnel, previews, defaults), `invariants.rs` (the speeds chain: passes 1, 9, 10 and the warn-only passes), `axial_envelope.rs`, `adaptive_entry.rs`, `tests.rs`.
- `geometry.rs`, `geometry_class.rs`, `cutter_constraints.rs`, `force.rs`, `predict.rs`,
  `efficiency.rs`, `operating_point.rs`, `engagement.rs` — effective diameter, chip thinning, the DOC scale, the axial
  envelope, the arc and the feed-cap refusals (`force.rs`), deflection, efficiency, and spindle power at the shipped operating point, and the K7 `Engagement` (`from_cut`, `from_sample`), the private-field `PowerModelInputs`, `PowerCase` and `PowerCases`. The move-count model and the
  axial-envelope class are registry capabilities (W2.4): read `capabilities()`, add no op `match`. K1/K2 (P4, P5): the calculator inputs come from `CutSpec::calc_inputs` (the stated cut, through `operation_feeds_hints`); `FeedsHints` and `PresentBasis` are gone.
- `vendor_lut.rs`, `vendor_lookup.rs`, `vendor_normalize.rs` — vendor table, lookup, type mapping. `series.rs` — the row series (`SERIES_RULES`, `row_series`) and the lookup tiers. `ramp.rs` — G6 ramp: `RampBasis`, the entry θ, `resolve_ramp_feed`, the G10 `entry_notes`. `plunge.rs` — G10: `PlungeBasis`, `resolve_plunge`.
- `extrapolation.rs` + `extrapolation/{size,hardness,family,drill,plunge}.rs` — `SizeLaw` (G1 size claim, form A/B/C or refusal),
  `hardness_basis` (G2 Janka + soft/hard cap), `family_basis` (G3 `FAMILY_RULES`), `drill_basis` (G6 `DRILL_RULES`) run in `build_result`; `plunge_rule` (G10 `PLUNGE_RULES`).
- `profile.rs`, `quantities.rs`, `provenance.rs`, `support.rs` — the pre-sim profile (P5: it carries the `envelope`), the boundary newtypes, the per-value provenance (P5: a projection of `FeedsResult::bases`; P10: the slots `peck_depth` and `z_step`, which only the tool rule `compute::geometry_default` stamps; `suggest::default_operation_for_tool` is the add-door op with those rule values), the `FeedsSupport` arm. `sourced.rs` (K2) — `Sourced<T>`, `Basis`, `PointBases`, the unit newtypes, `lower_to_cap`. `envelope.rs` (K2b) — `SafeEnvelope::for_cut`, `Limit`, `SpeedsMap`, `Placement`, `PickRequest`, `EnvelopePick`; the grid size `ENVELOPE_GRID_N` and its time budget. P11a (L4): a grid point and a pick are at the depth they name (`calculate_with(.., DepthClamps::DrawnAsLimits)`: the flute guard and the slot cap are limits, not a clamp; D-A); the deflection edge is the solved shallowest depth (scan + bisection up to the flute), a column with no root takes the span top, and no root anywhere is `Unmodelled` "no deflection limit below the flute" (D-B). `lock.rs` — W3k `FeedsLock` (GUI only; a raw param write ignores it). `rationale.rs`, `feed_explanation.rs`, `explain_payload.rs` — rationale tree, stage record, UI contract.

## Invariants
- P11b (chip first, D11): Step 5 sizes the feed so the PEAK chip is the band chip (`geometry::peak_chip_thinning_lift`, Garr, no cap; `FeedsDerates::radial_chip_thinning`); a `PassRole::Finish` op and a drill cycle do not thin (P11b2: the one rule `geometry::RadialThinning`, read by Suggest, `efficiency`, pass 9, the gate and the modulator); the feed basis names "radial chip thinning (Garr)" or "finish pass: no chip thinning". `doc_derating_scale` scales neither the feed nor `ChiploadBounds` (the printed band at every depth); the envelope draws it as `LimitKind::VendorDepthTable` at the peak chip (`vendor_depth_table_ceiling_ratio`; no edge at or below half the band max). P11b2: no reader de-rates (the gate and the modulator band read the printed band too); `validate_chipload_bounds` (was `derate_chipload_bounds`, no ratio) is the one validation; `doc_derating_scale` stays only as the printed table that `vendor_depth_table_ceiling_ratio` inverts. The force edges and the depth table read the operator's chip (stated feed / (rpm x Z)), else the grid chip. `calculate` ships a STATED depth above the flute guard or slot cap (speeds at that depth; the warning names the limit); only a default depth is lowered. A V-bit row is read at its printed key, never the engaged width (ruling B4).
- One Janka table (`WoodSpecies` generics); no hardness scale on plywood/MDF; in solid wood an upward scale stops at the row family's printed soft/hard max.
- G6 (B5): no drill rows, no drill multiplier. `DRILL_RULES` (Amana Spektra flat, 3.0-12.7 mm, 2/3 F, widened 2026-09-25) read the (Pocket, Roughing) side row x 1/Z (a point stays a point); the drill RPM cap replaces the chart's 18 000; every other drill cell refuses, in every material. G6 ramp: `FeedsResult::ramp` holds the axial chip; `suggest::apply` writes `ramp_feed_rate = floor(min(shipped feed, chip x shipped rpm x Z / tan θ))`; no clamp rule.
- G10: plunge = the family fraction x shipped feed inside `PLUNGE_RULES` (flat 1/Z on the G6 range, ball and tapered 0.50, 60° V-bit 1/3), else the named material base; ball and tapered tip cap 150/mm; the funnel re-runs it at the shipped feed and RPM. Q8 (P8, lead decision Q8a: Project Curve only): an op whose entry contract is `StraightPlunge { feed: PlungeFeedRule::AxialChip }` also takes `G6 axial chip x rpm x Z` (`plunge::AxialChipBound`, binding `PlungeBinding::AxialChip`) where a G6 claim covers the tool (`RampBasis::Sourced`); a ball, tapered, bull or V-bit keeps the G10 rule. Non-G6 ramp = floor(min(F, plunge / tan θ)); a straight plunge or unknown entry writes `None`. Suggest never writes the entry style (Q11). Part B: θ and the helix notes read the emitted r (`helix_radius_for`: rule 0.3 x the nominal D, `helix_entry_diameter_mm` (a tapered ball's tip, not its shaft), capped at the flat bottom), so the card states the cap, not a core.
- Suggest is the validated application path; a raw parameter write is an override and stales the result. On an adaptive rough the simulated chipload outranks the Suggest verdict. K2b (P5, ruling Q4): Suggest writes geometry only on a pick. `apply` writes the speeds for the geometry the op keeps (G-SPEEDSAPPLY: the geometry passes 0, 2-6, 6b do not run; passes 1, 9 and 10 read the kept geometry); a `Recommendation` from an `EnvelopePick` first writes the picked depth and width (the depth snaps once; a drill takes it as its peck), stamped `Manual` "picked". The add door (`suggest_for_operation`, D1: a registry default is a stated value) writes the speeds only. The calculator reads the stated cut (`CutSpec::calc_inputs`). The proposal is `min(stated, binding cap)` and never raises (the cap API `lower_to_cap`); a drill peck proposal is `min(stated, the drill peck rule)`. The geometry passes of the old chain (0, 2-6, 6b) and its test doors are deleted (lead ruling 2026-11-03): no sentry reads a path production does not run; the envelope holds their limits. F114: `apply` is THE write; `apply_to_operation(input, pick, explored, ..)` is the project funnel of `Command::ApplyFeeds`; every apply door calls `accept_unsourced` (D4). The pills read `preview_field_applies`, the dry run of the proposal pick.
- K7 (P9): one engagement. Every power reader (calculator Step 6 and the published `power_kw`, `power_at_operating_point`, the gate `sample_power_kw`, the modulator power cap) builds `PowerModelInputs` through `Engagement`; P11a (L5): `Engagement::edge_force` sums `Ks h + F_edge` over the engaged edge with the helix lag (`tool::helix_lag_rad`, exact in z, `EDGE_SAMPLES_PER_PITCH` rotations): the mean is power's (`PowerTerms`, equal for a flat end mill), the PEAK resultant is the lateral force of every deflection reader (predictor, gate, envelope, inspector, modulator; `fz_cap_for_peak_force` is the one feed cap); `K_r = 0` (no source). The one-tooth model is deleted. Every reader builds the cut at `d_eng` of the cut depth (`from_tool_cut`, or `effective_diameter`). Pre-sim power is the maximum over the `PowerCases` that the `CutSpec` admits (`FeedsInput::power_cases`, built by `feeds_input_for_operation`; a caller with no op states `PowerCases::NOMINAL`); the result names its `PowerCase` (`FeedsResult::power_case`, `PowerFigure::case`), the Step 6 ladder and the Step 7 power check read the maximum, and the envelope power law names the case. A `Radial::Slot` op (Profile, Trace, Project Curve, Bore) states its width D, so `power_at_operating_point` no longer refuses it `NoRadialEngagement`. Q5: Suggest reads no simulation; only `engagement.rs` names `SimulationCutSample` (sentry `the_simulation_is_placed_not_fed_back_k7`).
- R4: the rubbing floor `min(0.025, band min)` warns, never lifts; no machine or L/D feed factor. D9 (2026-11-04): the machine aggressiveness dial is removed; the project and machine files refuse the key (`the_retired_aggressiveness_key_refuses_with_its_name_d9`). Machine rigidity is `MachineProfile::rigidity`; P11c (D13 item 3): the slot cap is `rigidity.slot_doc_factor` (`slot_rule(machine)`, read by Step 4b and the envelope `Slot` limit; the 0.85 D threshold stays a geometry rule); every rigidity-set value takes `feeds::rigidity_basis(machine)` (a `Derived` whose law is the class text, over `machine.rigidity`). P11c (M1): `DEFLECTION_BACKOFF_TARGET_UM` is the one typed 200 um (the F201 row, `DEFAULT_ROUGH_DEFLECTION_LIMIT_UM` and the gate `EXCEEDS_BOUND_MM` read it); (M4) the predictor fallbacks read `default_radial_width` and the machine `adaptive_woc_factor`. P11c held item 2 (the Pocket add-door stepover from the envelope): no computed limit binds the width below D at the add-door depth (`the_pocket_width_has_no_computed_limit_below_d_p11c`, an inverted claim). Power ceiling = `power_at_rpm`, no fraction. Q1 (P8, replaces R4 Q10 at the cap): when the cutting-feed cap binds, Step 7 builds arm L ("lower rpm, same chip": `next_rpm_at_or_below(floor(cap / chip per rev))`, not under the row or machine floor) and arm S ("same rpm, smaller chip"), both at the cap; the demand of each is `cap_arm_demand_kw` (the K7 worst case), the available power `power_at_rpm`. One inside ships; both inside ship L; neither inside ships the smaller exceedance with one `PowerLimited`. No bisection walk. `RpmLoweredForFeedCeiling::arms` (`CapArms`) holds both arms; the RPM and feed bases name the arm (`CapArm::law`).
- B6: force, power, efficiency and the deflection predictor read one line, `Material::force_line()`; power is `line × grain_factor` (1.0 on every line), deflection the line alone. No `Kc`, no anchor. A refusal is `MaterialUnvalidated`; a chip outside the printed range extends the line and `PowerFigure::force` names the `ChipRegime`.
- G3 (A3): a chart that names no operation gives one row per printed cell under (Pocket, Roughing); `FAMILY_RULES` (tapered: Onsrud 77-100; bull: Amana corner radius) serve adaptive, contour, parallel, scallop and trace at x1.00; the Amana ZrN v8 tapered rows (home (Parallel, Finish)) serve contour, scallop and trace (ruling 2026-10-01). No copy rows, no ball rule (B3). `ProjectCurve` routes ball, tapered and bull to (Parallel, Finish), and a V-bit to (Trace, Finish) (operator ruling 2026-09-25); a facing bit still refuses. Q3 (P5, D2): on a tie after the score, the diameter, the transfer and the series rules, the lower printed chip wins, then the id; `LookupResult::tied_with` names the other row (MCP `basis.tied_rows`). Series (rulings 2026-10-31): `series::row_series` marks compression, downcut, ZrN carving, O-flute, single-flute and chipbreaker rows; the series is a tie-breaker (`vendor_lookup::prefer_series`): where rows tie on material family and printed size, the tool's own series wins, then a standard row (ZrN tapered rows stay standard, G3); the score decides every other choice. Both resolvers pass through it; the cut direction declares the tool's series.
- S11: `StockContext::stock_z` is `None` for a flat stock (no thickness): no through floor. `apply_stock_defaults` writes no Pocket or Adaptive 2D floor. With `Unbacked` the add has no feeds (no raw fallback); `FM1` states the pre-S11 add op (`add_time_op`). One claimed band: every consumer reads `LookupResult::size_basis`; a `Refused` row has no band anywhere. A2: one printed value is a point (`printed_chipload()`); no band is derived; the modulator caps at it with no floor; the gate is hard above it and advisory below it. FC0 (decision D3, 2026-11-03): a row whose `source_page` prints `a" - b" IPM at N RPM` holds a chip band that meets `[a, b] / (N x Z) x 25.4`. The Amana ZrN 2F 1/16" cell prints a chip twice its own feed; its three rows hold the band of the printed feed (reverses P1 decision 3). Sentry `every_printed_chip_band_meets_its_printed_feed_fc0`.
