# `dressup/` — post-generation transforms

The passes run after an op generates a toolpath. `apply_dressups` in
`compute/execute/dressup_apply.rs` drives them from a `DressupContext`;
`DRESSUP_PIPELINE` there lists every `DressupStep` in run order (F128: one fn and one exhaustive arm per step). Only these run outside
it: `apply_tabs`, in `ProfilePass::emit_level` of `compute/execute/clearing_2d.rs`
(each level below the tab top lifts, P5b), and `optimize_entry_descents_annotated` and
`adaptive_feed_modulate_with_materials`, in `session/compute.rs` (prior stock, engagement). 3D Rough calls the emitters `emit_helix` and `emit_ramp`. P29-8e (Q8): the fold walks `fold_walk_length` = the budget and at least `fold_min_run_mm` (the gate), so one cut gives one entry whatever its move count (`--lib a_fine_polyline_and_one_arc_give_the_same_entry_p29_8e`). Review F1: the followed cut ends at a rise (a tab lift), and no fold point or chord goes below it (`--lib no_fold_ramp_point_is_below_the_followed_cut_review_f1`). P29-8e: the entry step after the clips (`compute::execute::enter_clip_reentries`, called from `session/compute/clip_entry.rs`) runs the shared `apply_entry_at` on the clip re-entries only (`EntrySites::ClipReentries`), with `ResolvedEntry::clip_reentry` (Q9: under Helix the fold ramp; Q10: never with a surface probe, so only the fold). Add no other pass outside it.

## Files

- `mod.rs` — the configuration and the facade. `entry_descent.rs` — the ramp and helix emitters. `entry_audit.rs` — a fed move's burial audit.
  `lead_side.rs` — the side of a lead arc (P6.0c, SAFETY): away from the `RingMaterial` the op states per closed pass (`Produced::closed_passes`); an arc off the air side keeps the plunge; no side and no probe: no lead.
- `link.rs` — link against retract. `arcfit.rs` — lines to arcs, within the
  tolerance in 3D (G-TIERBURIAL). `feedopt.rs`, `feed_modulation.rs`,
  `air_cut.rs`, `tsp.rs`. `tabs.rs` — tab placement on straight sides (P5), the lift from the bridge width (P5b) and `apply_tabs`; P4.6: `plan_profile_tabs` is THE placement (`ProfilePass::prepare` and the `tab_layout` query; it reads `ops::profile`; P35: it returns the `RadialLeaveRefusal` of a loop that `stock_to_leave_radial` collapses, and the tabs sit on the moved ring; P35 D: a split loop gives one contour per ring, the first ring is the ring of the region (hand tabs, the drawn outline), a later ring has no region and takes automatic tabs by its own sides) and `resolve_hand_tabs` the one hand-tab resolver. `condition.rs` — segment merge (roughing; off where
  `planner_applies_segment_merge`: 3D Rough, 2D Adaptive; G-PLANSIMGAP; P6.0b: on a `finished_edge` op at most `FlattenPolicy::UNTOLERANCED_MM`).

## Invariants

- G-RETRACTDIAL is closed (CUT-03): the retract COUNT is the lever. `filter_air_cuts` bridges an air run only when `machine::kinematics::retract_saves_time` says the retract is faster (10-15); its clearance is `AIR_CUT_CLEARANCE_MM` (0.1 mm; P35 G: the flute-length lift of a Project Curve reads it as its margin).
- Every linking arm calls the shared `relink_fragments` kernel in `finish/surface_link.rs`. Do not add a second linking implementation.
- `ModulationContext::cutting_ceiling` is `CuttingCeilingMmMin`, made only by `MachineProfile::cutting_ceiling` (F002). F195: the folder denies `cast_possible_truncation` and `cast_sign_loss`; a count from a user length goes through `util::steps`, or its `#[expect]` reason names the bound.
- A helix or ramp starts `entry_clearance_mm` over the material and takes the full depth at `ramp_feed_rate`; air is straight (rulings 09-24/25). A `helix_floor_lap` helix (2D Adaptive) ends on a flat lap and shrinks to fit `entry_containment`, arc fit inside it; G-SIMCORE: under the core floor (`helix_fit_holds`, the 2D planner reads it too) it ramps and records a `HelixCoreFallback` (K5 `HelixCore`); `OwnStockReplay` and `feedopt` stamp `tool::body::cut_table`; under entry None a `stock_aware_plunge` op rapids through air first (G-ADAPTPASSLOAD). G-CENTRECUT: `EntrySafety::core` is the body core zone (`tool::body::core_of`; its methods are the rules). With it the emitters clamp the ramp angle and the helix pitch to the core rules, the rest ladder (`pencil::plan_entry_ramp`) takes a window of at least `d_core` and laps at `p / 4c` (P22-0c-2; P22-0c-3: no lap cap, the count follows from the depth), ignore `contact_top` (the conservative top only), and return a `NonCentreCuttingRefusal` for a fallback plunge deeper than the protrusion; `apply_entry` puts it in `Produced`. K5 P7 C: under the G-RAMPTERRAIN clip a `No` tool re-plans the ramp over the clip floor at its limit slope (`replan_descent_at_limit`: only up, never below the floor; other directions before the plunge fallback). P29-8: no emitter holds an entry number; the angle (8b: stated, or the rule of the stock class through `DressupContext::material`), radius, pitch, fallback angle (`EntryStyle::Helix::fallback_ramp_angle_deg`, the op's own angle) come from `feeds::entry_rule::ResolvedEntry`; the plunge feed is the op plunge rate (8c, `DressupContext::plunge_rate_mm_min`) (sentry `one_entry_rule_two_emitters_p29_8`).
- K4 (feeds contract P3): the air lead above an entry is `MoveIntent::EntryApproach` (`rapid_to_entry_top`, the `emit_ramp` lap ladder, the G-ISOCLIPENTRY rest ramp in `optimize_entry_descents`, the Pencil crease ramp). - P11b2: the band holds the PEAK chip. `ModulationContext::radial_thinning` (`RadialThinning::of` the op's feeds style, set in `session::compute`) gives the lift at the move's modelled engagement (`woc x d_eng`); the ConstrainedMax chipload cap, floor and pin arms are `band x lift x rpm x Z`, and the reach probe runs at the cutting ceiling, so the lift is not clipped. `BandMid` and `feedopt` read the same lift (P14, review M2; `BandMid` was `1/sqrt(woc)`, `feedopt` a copy clamped to [1, 4]).

P18 (D15): a ConstrainedMax move with no engagement and a bandless move take `no_engagement_feed`: under the ceiling and under the reach of a probe at the cutting ceiling (the engaged probe runs at the band ceiling); engaged moves read the engaged probe only (sentry `a_move_with_no_engagement_stays_inside_its_reach_p18`). C38 §15: `Move::feed_cap` binds last in every feed stage (modulator, feedopt) and only lowers; arc fit and segment merge join only moves with equal caps.
- P29-10z (ruling Q2), P30-2: `RunModulation` (`Off`, `Current { strategy, feed_scale, held }`, `NotRun { export }`, no default; `held: HeldByResult` has no public constructor, so only core builds the softening value; tests use `current_for_tests`) is the modulation of a toolpath's stored result, built only by `ProjectSession::run_modulation_of` (the provenance records the strategy and scale; `NotRun` reads `DEFAULT_RUN`'s list); `capped_limits` is THE declared list of the limits whose ROW bound the modulator holds, so a row of a member can soften (`ChipMax` only, in cap only and in full; empty at a feed scale over 1; P32: it is NOT the list of the modulator's caps, which `BindingConstraint` names: chip maximum, deflection at 200 um, power, the force budget only while it gates, the machine ceiling, the reach, the plunge rate, a planner cap), `re_times(CutKind)` the cuts it re-times (by `intents_of` against `should_skip_modulation`; the link and the peck ladder are not decided: `false`).
