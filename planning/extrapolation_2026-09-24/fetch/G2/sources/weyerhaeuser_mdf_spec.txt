Specifications for Standard MDF Products
  Weyerhaeuser Super-Refined MDF2® is an engineered wood panel prized by woodworkers for its exceptional qualities, including a smooth,
  unblemished surface and homogeneous core. Our products are made from high-quality inland species of western softwoods known for their low
  abrasive content and light color, providing for unsurpassed ease of routing and finishing.


      Weyerhaeuser Glacier GreenTM Meets the Following:
            • Fiber sourcing requirements of the Sustainable Forestry Initiative® (SFI)
            • EPA TSCA Tile VI & CARB ATCM 93120
            • The Eco-Certified Composite (ECC) Standard 4-11
            • LEED® credit support under LEED 2009 and LEED v4
            • The ICC/ASHRAE 700 – 2015 National Green Building Standard
            • Green Globes® Rating System
