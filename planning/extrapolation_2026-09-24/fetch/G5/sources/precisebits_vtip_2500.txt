Think & Tinker V-groove scoring and engraving cutters are built around a classic V-tip geometry enhanced with revisions to the cutting edges and flute profile. These changes result in bits with improved cutting properties in a wider range of materials. Our tools are routinely used to shape and engrave metals, plastics, all types of woods and a wide variety of composites.

Manufactured in the USA, every tool is:

100% measured / optically inspected to insure that EVERY tool meets, or exceeds, our published specifications

available with depth rings precisely positioned to ±0.0015 in. (0.038mm), virtually eliminating the need for resetting Z-axis zero between tool changes (with bits of same overall length)

made from premium grade submicrograin tungsten carbide (HRa 92, HRc 80)

This attention to detail and the use of top quality materials result in tools with dependable cutting characteristics and one of the longest tool lives in the market. Combined with comprehensive customer support, our cutters have become the tools of choice for sign makers, model makers, artists and instrument builders around the world.

Geometry - 2-flute, V-tip point

Material - solid sub-micrograin carbide

Operating RPM - CNC certified operation 4k RPM to 40k RPM

Side-lit plastic signs - Tip sharpness and dimensional fidelity insure that engravings into transparent and translucent thermoplastics cut clean, with out the debris that plague many other v-tip tools. This is especially critical in very fine (0.20 in. high) text and complex line art.

Wooden signs and complex contours - Holding a tight tolerance on the V-tip side angle allows these tools to be used in multi-pass, deep engraving of text and dimensional signage.

Plaques and awards - Multi-material compatibility insures that no tool change is required when cutting from brass into wood into thermoplastic into most other materials you are using for your awards. Clear, crisp engraving insures a professional product every time.

Custom edging and chamfering - Pre-chamfering of complex shapes (like monograms) eliminates the need for time consuming, and variable hand sanding. The ability to create curved and wavy edge profiles provides a more finished look to all of your cutting and contouring projects.
