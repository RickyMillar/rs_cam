1550

60° V-Groove Bit

1/4"SH, 1/2"CD, 7/16"PL

Product Description

Materials: Natural woods, composite woods, hard plastics, thin aluminum

Recommended RPM: 16,000-20,000 (max 24,000)

V-Groove bits are used for a variety of projects where a standard v-point cut is desired. V-Groove bits are available in 60, 90, and 120 degree tips.

TOOL SPECS: Carbide-tipped, 60° included angle v groove bit with 1/2" cutting diameter, 7/16" point length, 1/4" shank, and 2" overall length for use with 1/4" collets.

V GROOVE: Perfect for creating V-shaped grooves, engraved lettering, sign making, adding decorative accents to furniture, and carving detailed designs on flat surfaces with precision.

CARBIDE TIPPED: Offers an excellent balance of quality and cost, making it ideal for a wide range of applications.

MATERIALS: Perfect for use on natural woods, composite woods, and hard plastics, providing clean and precise cuts across a variety of surfaces.

DISTINCT PROFILES: Designed for hand-held routers, router tables, and CNC machines in a wide variety of applications.
