Tooltip text on the "Specific Gravity" term (the site's own definition of
its two printed numbers; the same tooltip text appears on every species
page):

"Technically, specific gravity is a measure of the ratio of a wood's
density as compared to water. (So if a wood is of the same density as
water, the specific gravity would be 1.00.) The first number is the basic
specific gravity, based on the botanical standard of ovendry weight and
green volume. The second number is meant for woodworkers, and is simply a
snapshot of the wood's specific gravity at 12% MC, (that is, both 12% MC
weight and volume)."
