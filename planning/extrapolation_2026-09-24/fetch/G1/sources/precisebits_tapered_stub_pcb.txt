2-flute and 3-flute tapered stub end-mills are designed around a 15° tapered core and flute profile. This geometry results in tools that are as stiff and durable as conventional V-tip cutters while offering superior copperclad substrates for printed circuit board (PCB) fabrication and engraving of both ferrous and nod-ferrous metals.
Manufactured in the USA, every tool is:
100% measured / optically inspected to insure that EVERY tool meets, or exceeds, our published specifications
available with depth rings precisely positioned to ±0.004 in. (0.10mm), virtually eliminating the need for resetting Z-axis zero in many applications)
made from premium grade submicrograin tungsten carbide (HRa: 93, TRS: 4.0GPa )
This attention to detail and the use of top quality materials result in tools with dependable cutting characteristics and one of the longest tool lives in the market. Combined with comprehensive customer support, our cutters have become the tools of choice for sign makers, model makers, artists and instrument builders around the world.

0.0039" (0.10mm) dia - multiply your peak spindle RPM by 0.00012 (dry) or 0.00016 (with ME Lube ) with 5 IPM z-axis plunge rate
0.0051" (0.13mm) dia - multiply your peak spindle RPM by 0.00069 (dry) or 0.00131 (with ME Lube ) with 10 IPM z-axis plunge rate
0.0071" (0.18mm) dia - multiply your peak spindle RPM by 0.00180 (dry) or 0.00233 (with ME Lube ) with 10 IPM z-axis plunge rate
0.0100" (0.25mm) dia - multiply your peak spindle RPM by 0.00254 (dry) or 0.00328 (with ME Lube ) with 20 IPM z-axis plunge rate
0.0150" (0.38mm) dia - multiply your peak spindle RPM by 0.00381 (dry) or 0.00492 (with ME Lube ) with 20 IPM z-axis plunge rate
0.0200" (0.51mm) dia - multiply your peak spindle RPM by 0.00508 (dry) or 0.00656 (with ME Lube ) with 20 IPM z-axis plunge rate
0.0250" (0.63mm) dia - multiply your peak spindle RPM by 0.00634 (dry) or 0.00820 (with ME Lube ) with 20 IPM z-axis plunge rate
For example. Using a 0.0051" dia bit at 24,000 RPM with ME Lube would allow a feed-rate as high as: (24,000 RPM X 0.00131) = 31.4 IPM
