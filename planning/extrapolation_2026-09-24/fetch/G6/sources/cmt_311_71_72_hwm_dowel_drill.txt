Solid Carbide Dowel Drills - LONG LIFE SHARPENING, S=10mm - L=70mm

Solid Carbide Dowel Drills - LONG LIFE SHARPENING, S=10mm - L=70mm

311.71/72HWM

Technical details:

Premium quality super strength steel shank for improved resistance and durability

Flute length in premium quality HWM

2 cutting edges [Z2] + 2 curved ground spurs [V2]

2 spiral flutes

Parallel shank with driving flat and adjustable screw length

Recommended feed speed 1÷ 4m/minute – RPM 6000.

CMT’s new high quality dowel drills guarantee excellent performance and extra-long life!

Details

Ideal for chipboard, MDF, HDF and laminates.

No center-point or spurs means perfect bores in low-thickness panels.

For use on boring machines equipped with adapters/chucks.
