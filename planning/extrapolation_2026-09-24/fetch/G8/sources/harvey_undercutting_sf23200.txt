         Undercutting Guide


Undercutting end mills, often referred to as lollipop cutters, are extremely versatile tools. Harvey tool offers a variety of
reach and wrap angles to provide an answer for even the most difficult of applications. Due to the varying neck lengths
and the applications, specific machining parameters must be calculated to avoid breakage.




Speeds & Feeds calculations:
1. Determine the correct SFM and Base Chip Load (IPT) for the cutter, material and application (see application descriptions Figure
1.)
2. Adjust Chip Load to account for neck length to cutter diameter ratio. (see Table 2)
3. Calculate the Speed (RPM) and Linear Feed (IPM)
4. Determine correct number of passes
