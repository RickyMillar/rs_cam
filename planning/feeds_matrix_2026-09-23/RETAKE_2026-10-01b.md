The 16 moved cells are all `TaperedBallNose`, 2 flutes, MDF, on the
operations Profile, Trace, Waterline, Pencil, Scallop, UnifiedFinish,
SteepShallow and SpiralFinish, at two sizes.

| Size | Row before | Row now | Band before (mm/tooth) | Band now (mm/tooth) | Size basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3.175 mm | `onsrud-mdf-77-100-1_8-pocket` | `amana-tapered-mdf-parallel-3175-3f-zrn-v8` | 0.0762-0.1270 | 0.0381-0.0635 | Exact -> Exact |

Cause, one for all 16 cells: the new rule lets the v8 MDF rows compete on
the Contour, Trace and Scallop queries. In MDF the v8 rows are grade A,
exact, as the Onsrud MDF rows are. At the same diameter the two rows
score the same, both are transferred, and the id decides
(`vendor_lookup::beats`): "amana-..." sorts before "onsrud-...". At
3.175 mm both rows have 3 flutes. At 6.0 mm both anchors are 6.35 mm, 2
flutes; the v8 anchor has a 2-flute series (1.5875 and 6.35 mm) that
brackets 6.0 mm, so the claim is form A, not form C.

Columns that move with the row: rpm, feed, band, `r_chip_load_mm`,
`force_n`, `support_detail`, `lut_observation_id`, `lut_row_pass_role`,
`lut_source_id`, `family_home` (Pocket/Roughing -> Parallel/Finish),
`chipload_source`, `vendor_source`; on some cells `depth_per_pass_mm`,
`cap_mm` and `ramp_feed_mm_min`; at 6.0 mm the claim columns. The 8 cells
at 6.0 mm also gain `feeds.rpm_lowered_for_ceiling` /
`RpmLoweredForFeedCeiling`: the v8 row prints 18 000 RPM (`amana_zrn_3d_v8.txt:3`), and at
the larger band the feed reaches the feed ceiling, so Suggest lowers the
RPM from 18 000 to 10 125. Before, the Onsrud row (no printed RPM) gave
8000 or 12 100. That is the +8 in the summary.

No hardwood, softwood or plywood cell moved. In the solid woods the v8
rows are grade B, derived ("Wood" on the chart is not split into soft and
hard), so the Onsrud row (grade A, exact) wins at 3.175 and 6.0 mm. The
chart prints no plywood row. FM1 walks no tapered tip under 3.175 mm,
so the Ø1 Scallop cell that the ruling names is not in FM1; the sentry
`the_printed_tip_row_serves_every_3d_finish_g3v8` pins it.

No cell moved without a cause.
