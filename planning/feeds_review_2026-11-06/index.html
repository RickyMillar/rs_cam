Does the feeds engine cut like the printed charts say?

The reviewer wrote a safe cut for each toolpath from printed vendor and hobby charts first, then read Suggest, the envelope and the post-sim gate. Each disagreement names the missing term. Every number on this page comes from claims.json; assert_claims.py re-derives it from the engine output, the chart text or the code.

The lead finding: one chart, two chips

Amana Spektra prints one Wood/Plywood chip, 0.127 mm at 6 mm. For white oak the engine ships 0.1311 mm (rivmap100 "Face 5", 5550 mm/min against 5375 mm/min in softwood). The Spektra row carries an engine proxy Janka of 1450; white oak is 1360. The square root of their ratio is 1.0326. No chart prints the proxy.

The same proxy flips the row for a 6.35 mm 2F tool. Generic hardwood reads Spektra at 0.127 mm. White oak reads freud-solid-carbide-quarter-hardwood at 0.2492 mm and 10026 rpm. The chart below puts the printed bands beside the two engine chips.

Bands: Onsrud 52-200 0.127-0.178 mm; Freud 0.2032-0.2794 mm; Spektra one point; Sienci hobby reading 0.114 mm (2 flutes assumed, the chart prints none).

Disagreements

B

D1 · Face 5, white oak

Chip 0.1311 mm against the printed 0.127 mm. Missing term: a hardness span per row. A one-column chart has no slope inside its column.

A

D2 · 6.35 mm 2F, white oak

Row freud-solid-carbide-quarter-hardwood, 0.2492 mm at 10026 rpm. Missing terms: one reader of a missing hardness (the score gives 0 points, the transfer gives the family default; the hardness points break the Spektra-Freud tie before the lower-chip rule runs) and agreement across vendors.

A

D3 · R37, 3.175 mm hardwood

The proposal is 6.985 mm deep and 2.32 mm wide at 0.0889 mm. Sienci prints 5.6 mm at 1.45 mm. The depth gate on the simulated cut gives a force-budget depth of 5.00 mm and exceeds, but it reads a different feed from the chip gate. Missing term: one feed for Suggest and both gates.

B

D4 · R36, hardwood slot

The 6.35 mm proposal is 3.39 mm at 0.85 D. Sienci prints 2.5 mm and Carbide 3D 2.54 mm. The engine separates hardwood from softwood by 1.76 per mm; Sienci by a depth ratio of 2.16. Missing term: a species factor beyond density.

A

D5 · Baltic birch plywood

No force line, so the 6.35 mm proposal is 10.58 mm deep. Sienci prints 2.5 mm for hard plywood. Missing term: a force line for plywood.

A

D6 · a 0.05 D width

Suggest lifts the feed by 2.29. The pocket path meets near-slot widths, and the depth gate gives a budget of 1.85 mm against 6.0 mm. Two terms can explain it: the engagement spread of the planned path, or one feed for both gates.

A

D7 · Project Curve depth

The cut spec reads the signed offset -4.0 mm as the depth; the calculator then uses 0.36 mm, a 0.06 D default. Missing term: the sign of the surface offset in the cut spec.

C

D8 · power ladder jump

The last power rung has no rubbing-floor bound. Only cells with a shank the machine cannot hold reach it.

C

D9 · the 6000 mm/min cutting cap

A typed default with no stored source and no F201 row.

B

D10 · 2 mm 1F lake pockets

The size law scales up from the 1.5 mm row and passes the printed 3/32 in row. Missing term: interpolation between bracketing rows.

C

D11 · V60 feed

The row stores the chip column only; the printed 90 in/min sits in a note. Missing term: the printed feed in the row.

C

D12 · the 0.25 D slot cap

An unsourced cap below both printed hobby slots; the conservative side.

B

D13 · R38, a light finish pass

Harvey prints Finishing at 0.764 x Slot-Rough at 0.1 D and 3 D. The thinned reading is 0.833; the engine's no-thin reading is 0.5. Missing term: thinning on a light finish pass.
