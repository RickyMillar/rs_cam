D5 · Baltic birch plywood

No force line, so the 6.35 mm proposal is {{ply635_ap}} mm deep. Sienci prints 2.5 mm for hard plywood. Missing term: a force line for plywood.

A

D6 · a 0.05 D width

Suggest lifts the feed by {{thin_lift}}. The pocket path meets near-slot widths, and the depth gate gives a budget of {{thin_gate_budget}} mm against 6.0 mm. Two terms can explain it: the engagement spread of the planned path, or one feed for both gates.

A

D7 · Project Curve depth

The cut spec reads the signed offset {{river_lineage_ap}} mm as the depth; the calculator then uses {{river_calc_ap}} mm, a 0.06 D default. Missing term: the sign of the surface offset in the cut spec.

C

D8 · power ladder jump

The last power rung has no rubbing-floor bound. Only cells with a shank the machine cannot hold reach it.

C

D9 · the 6000 mm/min cutting cap

A typed default with no stored source and no F201 row.

B

D10 · 2 mm 1F lake pockets

The size law scales up from the 1.5 mm row and passes the printed 3/32 in row. Missing term: interpolation between bracketing rows.

C

D11 · V60 feed

The row stores the chip column only; the printed 90 in/min sits in a note. Missing term: the printed feed in the row.

C

D12 · the 0.25 D slot cap

An unsourced cap below both printed hobby slots; the conservative side.

B

D13 · R38, a light finish pass

Harvey prints Finishing at {{harvey_ratio}} x Slot-Rough at 0.1 D and 3 D. The thinned reading is {{harvey_thinned}}; the engine's no-thin reading is 0.5. Missing term: thinning on a light finish pass.
