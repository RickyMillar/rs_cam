4. Claims this page makes

node check_claims.mjs runs the camera model below and checks each one.

  • C1 Zoom to the cursor keeps the point under the pointer fixed on the screen; the centre zoom moves a point at the target depth by its distance from the centre times |1/k − 1|, where k is the distance factor.
  • C2 Orbit about the cursor pivot keeps the pivot fixed on the screen through the drag; the fixed-target orbit moves it.
  • C3 Moving the pivot alone changes no pose: the eye and the look direction are the same before the first drag step.
  • C0o At the target depth, the orthographic image of a point is the perspective image. The orthographic half height is distance × tan(fov/2), as in camera.rs.
  • C1o Zoom to the cursor in orthographic projection keeps the point under the pointer fixed on the screen. The rule holds for a point at the target depth and for a point off it.
  • C2o Orbit about the cursor pivot in orthographic projection keeps the pivot fixed on the screen through a 20-step drag. The drag goes past the pitch limit.
  • C3o The projection-derived pan in orthographic projection moves the grabbed point by exactly the pointer step in pixels. The point depth and the viewport height have no effect.
  • C4 The preset tween starts at the old pose, ends at the new pose, and moves monotonically between them.
  • C5 The hover rebuild writes 120 bytes per triangle (three 36-byte vertices and three 4-byte indices): 15 835 440 bytes for the 131 962-triangle terrain.
  • C6 The probe numbers in section 3 are the numbers in probe/run.log.
  • C7 Each cited file:line holds the named symbol at HEAD.
  • C8 The pan rule distance × 0.001 per pixel equals the projection-derived rule only at one viewport height (828 px at a 45° field of view); at other heights the point under the pointer drifts.
