Descents into stock: fix11/audit.py (vertical fed descents at one XY that remove stock, pecks included). Lift-over descents: a keep-down-link span that goes down 1.0 mm or more and removes more than 0.1 mm³ (entries.py). Plunge entries: the plunge-entry spans of the trace. Planar turns: junctions of two moves that both travel in XY; a reversal turns more than 90°. Inside / outside: the junction point against the hole-filled part silhouette (Corne) or the terrain footprint x 20 to 220, y 25 to 225 (wanaka200). Plunge-feed share: fed vertical-down moves of program.nc at their F, over the op's program time (rapids at the machine max feed). Cycle: summary.json; the Corne clock has no acceleration (its machine profile has no kinematics block), the wanaka200 clock uses the machine model (C39). 3 to 9 mm band: stock more than 0.8 mm above the target, outside the silhouette, 3 to 9 mm from it (bands_d25.py rule). Strict plane: both sample ends within 0.01 mm of the plane Z.
