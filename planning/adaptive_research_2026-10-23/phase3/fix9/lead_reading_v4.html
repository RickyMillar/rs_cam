Correction to v3. The v3 reading said that the research arm has 60 plunges on Corne and 20 on wanaka200. That count is the plunge-entry spans only. The research arm also goes down vertically into stock in its lift-over links: 1195 times on Corne and 3578 times on wanaka200 (fix9 section 4). The v3 level images also drew each arc as its chord, so they showed a sparse ring that is not in the path.

  1. Vertical descents into stock are the main defect of the research arm. At a wrap change, the link lifts over the uncut stock, steps across, and feeds straight down at the plunge feed. On Corne D20 the median drop is 5.26 mm. contour_parallel has 13 such descents on Corne and 123 on wanaka200.
  2. C31 lowers the side load. The thick over-load falls from 4.74% to 0.85% on Corne and from 4.39% to 0.69% on wanaka200. contour_parallel reads 7.96% and 4.84%.
  3. The circle on Corne is cut. Inside the part outline, D20 leaves 1042 mm² more than 0.8 mm above the target after the first rough op. contour_parallel leaves 904 mm². The larger triage numbers are the band outside the case wall (G-OUTERBAND).
  4. Cycle time. Corne: D20 2760 s, contour_parallel 2022 s. wanaka200: D20 4832 s, contour_parallel 5750 s. All columns use the same feeds and stepdown.
  5. Hard bars. The research arm passes collisions, gouge and generation on both parts. contour_parallel fails the gouge row on Corne (2 lines) and the collision row on wanaka200 (86 rapid).
