# F1.1 — G-MCPTOAST: MCP toasts report the handler outcome

Finding: UX-R03-003 (`planning/ui_review_2026-09-09/results/R03/REPORT.md`),
R08 source track §8 (`results/W03/support/r08_source_track.md`).
Branch: `ui-fix/F1.1` from `ui-fix-2026-09-09`. Date: 2026-09-09 / 10.

## What was wrong

`RsCamApp::handle_mcp_request` (`crates/rs_cam_viz/src/app/mcp.rs`) pushed
the toast for a request BEFORE it called the handler. No arm read the
handler's reply. Nine toasts used the past tense, so a refused request
announced success. The live reproduction: `add_toolpath` Scallop on a flat
end mill, refused by the Suggest door, reply `ok: false`, screen reads
"MCP: Added toolpath 'Detail finish (flat tool attempt)'".

## Files changed

- `crates/rs_cam_viz/src/mcp_bridge.rs` — new `McpOutcome`
  (`Succeeded | Refused(String)`), `McpOutcome::from_json_response`,
  `McpOutcome::from_result`, `McpOutcome::notification`, and
  `mutation_error_json` (the refusal document, moved here from
  `app/mcp.rs` so the classifier and the handlers share one shape).
- `crates/rs_cam_viz/src/controller.rs` — `AppController::push_mcp_outcome`
  (cfg `mcp`): pushes the success text at Info, or `MCP: {refusal}` at
  Warning.
- `crates/rs_cam_viz/src/app/mcp.rs` — thirteen dispatch arms rewired
  (toast after the handler, from the reply); `mcp_load_project` and
  `mcp_save_project` return `(McpResponse, McpOutcome)` because their
  reply is plain text; `mcp_mutation_error` delegates to
  `mutation_error_json` and takes `field: Option<&str>` (nineteen callers
  drop a `.to_owned()`). No handler's behaviour or reply payload changed.
- `crates/rs_cam_viz/tests/mcp_toasts_report_outcome_g_mcptoast.rs` — the
  sentry (new, 369 lines).
- `planning/ui_fix_2026-09-09/reports/F1.1.md` — this report.

Thirteen are synchronous mutations and now go through `push_mcp_outcome`
AFTER the handler. Three are asynchronous compute hand-offs; their
progressive tense is true at push time and they are unchanged.

On a refusal every rewired arm now pushes ONE Warning toast,
`MCP: {handler refusal text}`, and no success toast. For the live case the
toast reads
`MCP: Cannot add toolpath: scallop requires curved tip (need ball|bull|tapered_ball; got Flat on Scallop)`.

Refusal shapes the classifier reads: `"ok": false` + `summary`
(`mutation_error_json`, every mutation handler), and a bare top-level
`"error"` string (`import_model`, and the same shape elsewhere). A
`MutationResult` with `ok: true` and advisory `warnings` is a success; the
advisories belong to the reply, not the toast.

## Sentry

`crates/rs_cam_viz/tests/mcp_toasts_report_outcome_g_mcptoast.rs`, 7 tests.

`RsCamApp` needs an `eframe::CreationContext`, so the dispatch arm cannot
be driven from a test. The sentry pins two layers instead:

- Source (2 tests): each of the thirteen toast literals in `app/mcp.rs`
  sits after its handler call in the same arm and its nearest preceding
  push is `push_mcp_outcome(`; the only bare `push_notification(` calls
  are the three progress toasts.
- Contract on real payloads (5 tests, real `AppController` with a silent
  backend): (a) Scallop on a flat end mill through the same Suggest call
  the handler makes, its `Err` wrapped in `mutation_error_json` exactly as
  `mcp_add_toolpath` does — no toast contains "Added", exactly one
  Warning, containing "Cannot add toolpath" and the refusal; (b) the same
  on a ball nose passes the door and a `MutationResult { ok: true }` reply
  yields the unchanged "Added" toast at Info; (c) `open_job_from_path` on
  a nonexistent path — no toast contains "Loaded", one Warning; (d) a bare
  `{"error": …}` is a refusal carrying its text; (e) a success reply with
  advisory warnings is not a refusal.

## Not done / observed, out of scope

- The dispatch arm itself is not driven end-to-end in a test
  (`RsCamApp` construction needs eframe); the source-level check stands
  in. A headless `RsCamApp` constructor would let the sentry call
  `handle_mcp_request` directly.
- Two side effects still run regardless of outcome and were left as they
  were, per "do not change any handler's behaviour": `AddToolpath`
  selects the last toolpath (on a refusal, the previous last one), and
  `SetToolpathParam` / `SetToolParam` record the highlight and selection
  before the handler.
- The progressive-tense success texts ("Adding setup", "Moving
  toolpath", "Importing") are now pushed after completion and read
  slightly off; wording was kept unchanged as instructed.
