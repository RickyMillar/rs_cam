## The evidence, and why this sentence in particular

`planning/ui_review_2026-09-09/results/W02/evidence/01_finish_defaults.png` is
a 1400×900 capture with the terrain finish selected. Cropped and enlarged
around the inspector header, it shows two lines running to the window's right
edge and stopping mid-word, with no second line under either:

```
☑ Show reach map   unreachable 70.0 % of 3D surface area, rim-eroded 1.5 mm · max gap 2.82
Tool load: Chipload clamped to the matched band ceiling: 0.0098 → 0.0232 mm/tooth. The whole derat
Gates: run simulation to evaluate
```

**The caution is the one that matters.** Its full text reads: "… The whole
derated band sits below the {global} mm/tooth chip-formation floor, so no feed
clears rubbing without exceeding the band — still expect burnishing. Use a
larger tool, a softer material, or accept the finish."
(`rs_cam_core/src/diagnostics/adapters/from_feeds.rs`,
`FeedsWarning::ChiploadClampedToFloor` with `band_capped_from: Some(_)`.)

Truncated at "The whole derat", the operator is left with a clamp, two
numbers, and no statement of what is NOT promised. **A clipped caveat does not
read as a partial sentence; it reads as an unqualified result.** The clause
that says burnishing is still expected, and the three alternatives, are
exactly the part that falls off the edge — the qualification is at the end of
the sentence because that is how the sentence is written, so clipping removes
the qualification and leaves the claim.

The reach line loses the same kind of thing. `grid_note` and
`over_statement_note` ARE the missing-guarantee sentences for that number —
the cell, the floor and the bar, and the statement that the grid OVER-states
when the tolerance sits below the discretisation floor (CLAUDE.md's reach-map
paragraph says to read `grid_note` first). In the capture the summary is cut
mid-value at "2.82" and `grid_note` is never drawn at all.

## What changed

All in `crates/rs_cam_viz/src/ui/properties/mod.rs`.

### 1. The reach readings leave the checkbox's row

`ui.horizontal(…)` gives its child layout `TextWrapMode::Extend`: egui's
`Ui::wrap_mode` returns `Wrap` only for a vertical layout or a main-wrapped
horizontal one, and `Extend` otherwise (`egui-0.34.3/src/ui.rs:696-721`). The
readings were bare `ui.label`s on that row, so they inherited `Extend`, ran
past the panel and clipped. That is the defect the review captured.

**What `Extend` on that row does not mean, corrected 2026-09-10.** An earlier
draft of this report said the readings were "unwrappable by construction, at
any panel width — no amount of `.wrap()` would have helped". That is wrong.
`Label` resolves its mode as `self.wrap_mode.unwrap_or_else(|| ui.wrap_mode())`
(`egui-0.34.3/src/widgets/label.rs:191`) and `Label::wrap()` sets
`Some(TextWrapMode::Wrap)` (`label.rs:63`), so **the label's own setting
overrides the row's** and a `.wrap()` there WOULD have wrapped. What it would
have wrapped at is `ui.available_width()`, which on that row is only what the
checkbox leaves — a narrow column, not the panel width.

So PLAN.md's prescribed `.wrap()` was **insufficient, not inert**, and the
reason these lines move into the vertical flow is the wrapping **width**:
there they wrap at the panel's full width, which is the readable outcome the
acceptance criterion asks for. The conclusion is unchanged; the mechanism
behind it is now stated correctly.

The checkbox keeps its row, and so do the two short status words
("reach: computing…", "reach: not measured" — under twenty characters, no
caveat, cannot clip). The three readings that carry a caveat, and the
`Failed(message)` line, move to the vertical flow below it as full-width
wrapped lines.

### 2. `wrapped_small_label`

A four-line helper: `ui.add(Label::new(RichText.small().color(c)).wrap())`.
It sets no width, truncates nothing and changes no text; the caller still owns
the colour, because these lines are colour-coded by severity. Its point is
that a sentence states its own wrapping instead of inheriting it from whatever
row it happens to be written into — which is precisely what made this defect
possible.

### 3. The tool-load caution wraps explicitly

`render_diagnostic_row`'s message went from `ui.label(RichText)` to
`ui.add(Label::new(RichText).wrap())`.

### 4. The validator sentences too

The `validation_errors` loop draws whole sentences ("Rest machining requires
an earlier enabled operation…") and now goes through the same helper.

sentry's `every_sentence_in_the_header_is_wrapped` pins it going forward by
refusing any bare `ui.label(` in the header whose text contains a full stop
followed by a space, which is the shape of a multi-sentence caveat.

`FeedsWarning` loop draws the same family of sentences through a bare
`ui.label` in the Feeds tab. It is the same shape of problem on a different
surface and outside this row's scope — recorded as a follow-on below.

  started, so **no rendered evidence was produced and none is claimed.** This

  same reason: the panel draws through `egui::Ui` and cannot be instantiated
  in a test. Visual confirmation is owed to V6.2.

**And one more thing this fix does not prove — stated plainly because it
matters to whoever runs V6.2.** The tool-load caution was ALREADY inside
`ui.horizontal_wrapped`, whose wrap mode is `Wrap`, and egui 0.34's
`ScrollArea::vertical()` gives its content a finite width
(`scroll_area.rs:757-770`, the `if true` branch: "better to wrap text … than
showing a horizontal scrollbar"). By the source alone that row should have
wrapped, and the capture shows it did not — and shows it running to the
*window* edge, past the panel's own inner margin. So the panel's content was
being laid out wider than the panel is.

The plausible mechanism is the reach row itself: an `Extend` row demands the
full width of its text, and a resizable side panel reports that as its content
width. That makes removing the header's only `Extend` sentence the primary
change here rather than a second cosmetic one — but it is a hypothesis I could
not test without running the GUI, and I have not claimed it as fact. If V6.2
finds the caution still clipped, the next place to look is the panel's width
negotiation, not the labels.
