Where the numbers come from. The force model is F = ap · (Ks·h + F_edge) with Ks = 49.95 N/mm² and F_edge = 5.30 N/mm, fitted to woodresearch.sk 2019/05/12. Power is A · (Ks·MRR + F_edge·ap·Vc·duty). Both match crates/rs_cam_core, checked by derate_levers.py.

The gantry push is not a prediction. No thrust model exists in the codebase — this is register item T-10. The feed force is computed from the engagement geometry as the peak of -(F_t·cos φ + K_r·F_t·sin φ) with K_r = 0.15 for softwood, which runs 0.53 to 1.0 of the tangential force. The thrust it is compared against comes from THRUST_RESEARCH.md: no maker publishes a rating, the belt figure is one hobbyist's fish-scale measurement, and the ballscrew and rack figures are derived arithmetic that nobody has checked against a machine.
