# P23c: an exact-size printed row outranks an interpolated band member (2026-11-10)

Scope from the lead brief: operator ruling 1 on wanaka idx 16 (`P23b_survey.md` section 4), in the
one vendor band rule (`vendor_lookup::VendorBand`, `BandRule`). Base `0c67ad7fe`. No commit, no
bless. Sections 1 to 5 were written before any code. The lead ruled (section 6); both parts are coded
and measured (section 7). The lead waived the stop condition for the 8 mdf cells; the pins are moved and FM1 is blessed
(section 8).

## 1. How the predictions were made

A temporary probe test (written, run once, deleted; not in the tree) printed the band of every
wanaka op (`ProjectSession::feeds_result_for_toolpath` on
`planning/rivmap_block_job/nz_wanaka_21_operator.toml`) and of every FM1 cell
(`tests/common/feeds_cells.rs::walk`): each member with its chip and its size basis class
(`SizeBasis::Exact` or a claim), the score winner, the rule and the shipped row.

## 2. The finding: the SpeTool row is not a member of the idx 16 band

- idx 16 (Scallop, tapered ball, tip 2.0 mm, 2 flutes, white oak) has a band of ONE member:
  `amana-tapered-hardwood-parallel-1587-2f-zrn-v8`, 0.06437, a form A claim. It is also the score
  winner. Rule `lowest`.
- `spetool-tapered-hardwood-parallel-2000-2f` (exact size, 0.0762, grade b, vendor class,
  2 flutes, hardwood) is not a member and not a candidate. The cause is not a band rule. It is
  the G3 family rule: `extrapolation/family.rs::FAMILY_RULES` lets a tapered-ball row serve the
  3D finish families (`SERVES_3D_FINISH`, Scallop among them) only from two sources: the Onsrud
  77-100 sheets and the Amana ZrN v8 chart (operator ruling 2026-10-01). The SpeTool rows print
  (Parallel, Finish) and have no transfer rule. A unit test holds that:
  `family.rs:460`, `assert!(transfer_rule(&scallop, &spetool).is_none())`.
- In the FM1 fixture a SpeTool row ships on 5 cells (DropCutter, Ramp Finish, Radial Finish,
  Horizontal Finish, Project Curve: the families that read the Parallel finish row itself) and on
  no Scallop cell.

So "an exact row outranks an interpolated member" is necessary and not sufficient for idx 16:
the exact row must first become a member.

### 3.1 A neighbouring rule that must NOT be folded in

21 FM1 cells ship a score winner that is not a member (`winner_ships`) while an `Exact` member
with a higher chip exists (for example Profile / EndMill 3.175 / hardwood: winner
`amana-flat-hardwood-contour-3175-2f` 0.0227, outside by grade; member
`onsrud-hardwood-60-100mw-1_8-finish` 0.2794 exact). If "exact outranks" reached past the
members to the non-member winner, those cells would rise by 0 % to +1130 % (4 cells by 0 %, 8 by +50 %,
5 by +154 %, 4 by +1130 %). The ruled rule is "among the members", so the no-rise rule stays.

## 4. What idx 16 needs (for the lead and the operator)

Two parts, in this order:

1. A G3 family rule for the SpeTool chart: the rows of
   `spetool_2d3d_tapered_router_bit_chart` (`tool_subfamily` `spetool_2d3d_tapered`, home
   (Parallel, Finish), printed tips 0.5 to 4.0 mm) serve the 3D finish families, as the Amana
   ZrN v8 rows do. The witness is the same kind: the chart ("2D/3D router bit feed and speed
   chart") prints one chip per tip diameter and material and names no operation. This is a new
   transfer rule, so it needs an operator ruling, as the v8 rule did (2026-10-01).
2. The P23c band rule (exact first among the members). With part 1 the idx 16 band holds the
   SpeTool row (exact, 0.0762) and the Amana interpolation (claim, 0.06437); the exact row then
   ships: chip 0.0762, Suggest feed 0.0762 x 2 x 24000 = 3657 mm/min (lift 1, P4a).

Blast of part 1, not measured: every Scallop, Waterline, Pencil and other 3D finish cell of a
2-flute tapered ball at a SpeTool tip size gains a member. The FM1 tapered tips are 3.175 and
6.0 mm; SpeTool prints 3.175 (0.1016, exact). So FM1 Scallop cells at 3.175 are expected to gain
that member and, with part 2, to change shipped row where the SpeTool value differs from what
ships today. I measure it after the ruling, before any bless.

## 5. Ruling 2 (no ball lead-angle lift, P4a): no code

P4a is what ships. `feeds/calculator/feed.rs::step5_feed`: "The axial term of a ball at a shallow
depth stays OBSERVED, NOT APPLIED: the charts print no rule for a depth below 1 x D". The value
is carried as `FeedsDerates::observed_axial_chip_thinning` (`feeds/mod.rs:824-826`), and
`combined_factor` leaves it out. The idx 16 feed 3089 = 0.06437 x 2 x 24000 has no lift.

## 6. Lead ruling (2026-11-10): (a), both parts

- Part 1: the operator's "yes" ("the SpeTool row printed at exactly that size wins for the 2 mm
  tapered ball") covers the family rule. The SpeTool 2D/3D tapered chart serves the 3D finish
  families on the same witness as the Amana ZrN v8 rule (one chip per tip and material, no
  operation named; the chart title names 2D and 3D carving). Recorded beside `FAMILY_RULES` as
  "operator ruling 2026-11-10"; the unit test at `family.rs` now holds the transfer.
- Part 2: exact outranks a claim among the members only. A score winner that is not a member is
  untouched.
- Stop conditions before a bless: a chip above the value an exact row prints for the tool size;
  a moved cell outside the tapered-ball 3D finish families; a move over +60 %.

## 8. Lead ruling on the measurement, and the final gates (2026-11-10)
