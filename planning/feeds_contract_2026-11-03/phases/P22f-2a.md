# P22f-2a: the D65 rule on every fed step of a geodesic cut (2026-11-10)

Agent phase; no commit. Design and binding rulings: `P22f-2_DESIGN.md` (section 8). Base: the
tree after P22-0c-6b lands. This file holds section 1 (predictions) and the designs of the probe
and the sentry, written before any crate edit.

Scope: option (b): in the 3D lift of a Geodesic Adaptive cut, read `step_load` on the 3D planner
stock for every fed step, not only for the steps the shape rule flags; over the pass ceiling `c`
and at most a slot, cap the step at `capped_step_factor` (`c / L`, the plunge-rate and outer-cap
limits); over a slot, split, as today. The test-support probe (ruling 4). The sentry
`a_geodesic_arc_end_reads_the_stock_p22f2`. The generation-time measure (ruling 2). The wanaka
arm with move 1612 (ruling 3).

### 1.5 Generation time

`step_load` samples each step every half planner cell (0.75 mm) over the disc's cells
(`pi (R / cell)^2` = about 113 at R 9, cell 1.5). Today it runs on the shape-split steps only.
After (b) it runs on every fed step of the geodesic cuts: about the cut length of idx 1 over
0.75 mm, times 113 cells. Predicted: release +5 % to +10 % on idx 1's core stage (about
0.4-0.8 s on 8.2 s), debug a similar fraction. If the measure is over 10 % (ruling 2), the read
is restricted to the steps that end within R of a lifted or split step of the same level.

## 2. The probe (ruling 4, test-only)

```text
#[cfg(feature = "test-support")]
pub fn debug_level_plan_probe(mesh, index, cutter, &Adaptive3dParams, cancel)
    -> Result<Vec<LevelPlanProbe>, Cancelled>

pub struct LevelPlanProbe {
    pub z_level: f64,
    pub cell_mm: f64, pub origin: (f64, f64), pub cols: usize, pub rows: usize,
    /// the level's floor per cell: max(drop-cutter + leave, z_level)
    pub floor_z: Vec<f32>,
    /// the cells the 2D plan of the level holds cut (the union of the
    /// pieces' replay grids, mapped to the planner stock lattice)
    pub plan_cut: Vec<bool>,
    /// the 3D planner stock top per cell after the level's emission
    pub tops_after: Vec<f32>,
}
```

- Built where the level loop already has both (`clear_z_level_slice`): the 2D replay grid of each
  piece (`geodesic::slice` replay, the same replay `feed_caps` runs) and the planner stock after
  the level. A `RefCell` in the context collects them only under `test-support`.
- The agreement read: a cell disagrees when `plan_cut` holds and `tops_after` stands more than one
  planner cell (the vertical read tolerance of the dexel grid) above `floor_z`. The sentry
  measures the disagreement area at the terrace foot.
- No product reader; the probe sits beside `debug_adaptive_3d_segments_for_f029_probe`.

## 4. Open questions

1. The cap source of a flat step capped by the 3D read. `CappedSplit3d` ("capped_split_3d")
   names the split rule's step; a flat step is not a split candidate. Recommendation: a sixth
   `FeedCapSource` member `StockRead3d` ("stock_read_3d"), so the readouts and the P22a classes
   tell the two apart; the alternative is to keep `CappedSplit3d` and widen its doc to "a step
   the 3D stock read admits at a capped feed".
2. A flat step over a slot splits into an entry (today's rule for a shape-split step). On a flat
   step that adds a re-entry in the middle of a level. Recommendation: keep the rule (a slot is
   the D65 bound); count the new entries in the wanaka arm and name them in the note.
3. Move 1612's class in the P22a readout. The P22f-1 readout reported no uncapped class with a
   sample this wide (17.76 mm at ap 10), so the P22a classes may put it under another class
   (phantom by its span, or a joint). Recommendation: the P22f-2a wanaka arm prints the exact
   class and the cap of moves 1610-1613 by index.

## 5. Lead rulings (2026-11-10)

1. A sixth cap source, `FeedCapSource::StockRead3d` ("stock_read_3d"), names a flat step that the

## 8. Findings and open questions

1. **The planner-simulation stock gap** (7.3): on idx 1 the widest remaining real cuts are steps
   whose load the planner reads at or under `c` on its own 3D stock while the simulation reads
   near a full slot (move 1621: ap 10, ae 17.76, 2.19 kW, uncapped; move 1620 read 0.356 against
   0.987). Recommendation: a read-only diagnosis next (the planner stock against the simulated
   stock at those moves, through the level probe extended to a per-move read). Candidates, not
   verified: the non-centre-cutting tool (D 18, core 8: P22-0a stamps only the blade ring in the
   simulation, while the planner stamps the full disc), or `step_load`'s strip binning and
   clearance on a draped step.
2. **The moved pins** (7.4): a capped flat step is a cut of its own, which moves the emitted
   geometry, not only the feed. Recommendation: bless the four with this reason, or (if the
   geometry must not move) carry a step cap without splitting the cut (a per-move cap on the
   emitted path). The second needs the emitter to cap single moves inside one cut.
3. **Claim 2's premise was wrong**: the 2D plan and the planner stock agree after the level. The
   P22f-3 floor pieces then remove a width in time order, not a leftover. Recommendation: P22f-3
   is still worth doing (it removes the wide arc ends rather than lowering their feed), but its
   sentry reads the per-step load, not the end-of-level agreement.
4. **Op 2's time** (+15 %) has a changed input (idx 1's new stock), so it is no measure of (b).
   Recommendation: no action; note it.
