### P20a harness fix (lead ruling)

`literature_matrix` was red on `flat_3mm_drill_oak` and `flat_3mm_drill_softwood`: the shipped
feed sat 1/D per mm under the new ceiling and the harness called it Edge (major). The apply floors
a feed to a whole mm/min, so a feed clamped to the ceiling ships as floor(ceiling x D). The runner
(`tests/literature_matrix/runner.rs`, `APPLY_FEED_FLOOR_MM_MIN = 1.0`) now counts a value at most
1/D per mm below the plunge-envelope ceiling as at the clamp; `CLAMP_EPSILON` is unchanged. 21/21
pass, 56 cells; only the two target cells changed verdict.
