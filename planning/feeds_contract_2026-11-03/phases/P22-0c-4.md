## 2. Design as built

- `stock/contact.rs`: `ZoneProbe::of(zone, cutter, rule, motion)` gives a
  probe for every contact zone of `MountedBody::zones()`:
  - fed: `Core` (ring rule, unchanged), `FluteTop`, `Shank`, `Holder`,
    `Shoe`; rapid: `Shank`, `Holder`, `Shoe` (the envelope arm covers the
    core and the flute top on a rapid).
  - Shadow rule: a zone whose bottom is at or above the flute top and
    whose radius is no more than the envelope radius gives no probe (the
    flute top on a fed move, the envelope on a rapid, meets the same wood
    first at the same sample). A wider zone reads only its ring outside the
    envelope radius. A zone below the flute top (FC0: stickout shorter
    than the flute) reads its whole disc.
  - A shelf (`ContactRadius::Shelf`) gives no probe: the H1 head check
    already reads it against the stock box (`CollisionKind::Stock`).
  - A rapid straight up is exempt (the body at a higher tip meets a subset
    of the columns it met below), as the envelope arm.
- `ContactRule::{PreMove, InMove}`, `SHIPPED_RULE = PreMove` (section 3).
  `DiscSweep` is the in-move rule of the zones at or above the flute top:
  a column that came within the cutter radius in this move before the
  sample takes the lower of its top and an upper bound of the flute cut
  (`z(e2) + h(R)` once the flutes reach it; for a core tool only the ends
  of the interval where the column is in the ring). The bound is never
  lower than the true cut, so the rule never drops a real contact.
  `override_rule_for_count` (test support) and
  `WalkChecks::mounted_with_rule` serve the count and the Q2 arm.
- Results: `BodyContact { move_index, position, zone, name,
  zone_bottom_mm, material_over_tip_mm, motion }` (`stock/collision.rs`);
  `SimulationResult::body_contacts` (`ToolpathBodyContact`), one per move
  per zone. A rapid contact also records a struck rapid
  (`rapid_collisions`, `project.rapid_collision`). The walk builds the
  checks from `entry.tool.to_assembly(&entry.head)` (`WalkChecks::mounted`).
  The S5 memo key of an entry now also hashes the head bands at its
  holder face (the walk reads them).
- Ruling Q1: `HolderCollisionCheck::MeasuredOnStock(n)`.
  `ProjectSession::holder_checks_with_stock` puts it in place of the mesh
  outcome for each toolpath that the stored simulation carved by the walk
  and is fresh for (a drill keeps the mesh outcome: its removal is
  analytic). `diagnostics_with_holder_collisions` and
  `triage_with_holder_collisions` apply it, so the project diagnostics,
  the triage and every caller of those doors (MCP, CLI `diag`) read it.
  The `project.holder_collision` verdict of a stock answer states the
  zones and the worst contact in the G-code frame (`body_contact_row`);
  the triage lists one row per fed contact with its zone and position.
  The export (`Gate::GuiConfirm`, "a holder or rapid collision of the
  simulation") pushes `project.holder_collision` for a fresh stock answer
  with contacts. The mesh check stays for the answer before a simulation
  and for `min_safe_stickout`.
- Lead ruling on P22-0c-3 open Q3: `finish/ramp_finish.rs`
  `core_ring_skip` records each matched ring of a core tool where
  `max_ring_slope` is 0 (`RampReachClamp::core_ring_skips`); the adapter
  moves them into `Produced::helix_core_fallbacks` with the new
  `EntryFallback::NoPass`, so the K5 rule `HelixCore`
  (`safety.helix_inside_core`) names them ("N ramp ring(s) hold r .. mm
  only, inside the core radius .. mm: ... the ring is left uncut, first at
  X.. Y.. Z.."). The helix-entry text is unchanged when no ring is in the
  list. `RampReachClamp` lost `Copy` (it holds the list).
- GUI: `SimulationChecks::body_contacts` is copied at adopt and reaches
  the GUI `ProjectEvidence` (fingerprinted). The GUI holder outcome still
  comes from its own mesh check (open question 2).

both. A level or rising move can lose no report under the in-move rule
(a column the flutes reached has its top at most `z + L` when it came in,
and `z` does not fall), so only a descending move can differ.

## 5. Finding changes

- No pinned finding of a test or a fixture moved: every sentry above is
  green with no edit to an expected value, and the fixtures give 0
  contacts under both rules.
- New by design: a fed contact of a shank wider than the envelope, of a
  hard holder band or of a hard shoe part with the live stock is
  `project.holder_collision`; a rapid contact of these zones is a struck
  rapid. A ramp-finish ring inside the core diameter is a
  `safety.helix_inside_core` row.
- With a fresh simulation the project diagnostics, the triage and the
  export read the holder count from the stock (`MeasuredOnStock`), not
  from the mesh. Where the model is inside the stock the stock count is
  at least the mesh count; the mesh count had a skip for a band no wider
  than the cutter, the stock count has the shadow rule.
- Edited tests (no expected value): 21 `SimulationResult` literals gain
  `body_contacts`; 7 `TriageInputs` literals gain `body_contacts`; h21 and
  pr8b read `RampReachClamp` by `clone` (no `Copy`).

## 6. Decisions (conservative where no ruling settles it)

1. Q2: the pre-move rule ships (section 3).
2. A shelf gives no stock probe; the H1 stock-box check keeps it.
3. A retract feed (`MoveIntent::Retract`, a lift through cleared air)
   gets no contact test, as before (it never had one; no case dropped).
4. A drill keeps the mesh holder outcome (the walk does not see a drill).
5. A stale simulation never supersedes the mesh answer.
6. A rapid zone contact is in both `rapid_collisions` (the count every
   surface shows) and `body_contacts` (the zone and the position).

## 7. Open questions

   does not apply `holder_checks_with_stock` (its triage reads

   Make the per-toolpath count read `holder_checks_with_stock` too?
4. MCP `inspect_collisions` lists no body contact (no `zone` entry for
   shank, holder or shoe yet).
5. `rivmap100_boundary_strike.toml` does not load (retired key since
