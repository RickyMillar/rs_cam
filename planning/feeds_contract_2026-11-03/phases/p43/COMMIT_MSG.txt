planning: P43: the one-pass promise on a fresh rough: the cause, the design, the measures, and the patch that is not landed

A record only. No code of P43 is in the tree.

The defect: "Back bulk rough 18 mm" of the Wanaka back (fresh stock, Geodesic
Adaptive, 4.166 mm per pass) cuts 9.0 mm deep on the shelf at the frame walls.

The cause, measured: the 2D plan of a level does not always cut to the limit of
its own tool-centre region. At levels 21.334 and 17.168 its last wrap stands
one 2D cell (1.52 mm) inside the limit, and the 2D cleanup adds nothing there.
Level 13.002 has another region shape, its plan runs on the limit, and the edge
of its disc takes the strip that two levels left. The P41 guard and the P42
wall pass make a fresh rough worse on its 1.5 mm planner cell (measured again
at 4.166 mm per pass: body 9.0 to 10.33 and 10.57 mm).

The patch (phases/p43/not_landed/p43_fresh_wall_pass.patch): a third variant of
the dial, `OnePassHold::FreshStock`. Each level of a fresh Geodesic rough ends
with a wall line one planner cell less three cut tolerances inside the raw
region boundary, at each wall deeper than one pass, with no guard and no read
of a strip.

Measured with the patch, setup "1 Back": the body row and the deepest sample of
id 1 fall from 9.0 to 4.166 mm; the replay pieces deeper than 4.966 mm fall
from 36 (6 953 nodes) to 2 (11 nodes); id 1 takes 801 s (689 s). The patch is
not landed because the replay bar is 0 pieces: 2 pieces stay at two corners of
the frame (id 1), and 1 piece of 2 nodes appears in id 2. Setup "3 Front" keeps
each row. rivmap100 keeps its G-code (no stored file has a fresh Geodesic
rough).

The note holds the decisions for the lead (section 8).
