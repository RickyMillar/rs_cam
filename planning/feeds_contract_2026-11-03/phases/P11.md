## Goal

For flat end mills first (other shapes later): the chip load is held in its band, the feed is
sized for the actual chip (chip thinning), and force, power, deflection and tool strength at
that chip bound depth x stepover. The envelope then shows the depth-stepover trade, and a
higher feed (a thicker chip) pulls the force limits in.

- "Maybe depth and stepover live in a more detailed combined model?" Yes: one

- "If we can prove that with chip thinning our calcs match the recommendations ... that model

## Lead decisions for steps 1-6 (2026-11-05, from step 0 and D11; L1-L3 confirmed by the operator 2026-11-05)

Two commits, one agent each, in order: **P11a** (steps 2 and 3) then **P11b** (steps 1, 4, 5, 6).

- **L1 (step 1, D11): Suggest applies radial chip thinning as a model.** The target is the peak
  chip h = fz sin(psi) for psi < 90 degrees (Garr, `CREDITS.md`; `feeds/geometry.rs`). The feed
  is sized so that the peak chip equals the band chip: fz = h_band / sin(psi) for psi < 90
  degrees, and fz = h_band at or above (ae >= D/2: Garr prints no change). One formula, no
  per-row fit. The basis names it (`Basis::Derived`, law "radial chip thinning (Garr)").
- **L2: finish ops do not thin.** Garr's finish row (2.5 % D) holds a low chip on purpose
  (step 0 C13, a finish-quality term). An op whose cut rule (K1) is a finish keeps fz = h_band.
  The basis says so. This is the only exception, and it is printed.
- **L3: no new cap constant on the thinning factor.** The existing limits bound the lifted
  feed: the machine cutting-feed ceiling (Q1 arms), power (K7 worst case), deflection, and the
  rubbing floor. If a limit binds, the card names it. A typed cap needs a printed source.
- **L4 (step 2): fix D-A and D-B.** D-A: the speeds grid sizes power at the requested ap (the
  slot cap and the flute guard are limits drawn on the envelope, not a silent clamp of the
  power input). D-B: the deflection edge is the solved ap where deflection meets its target;
  the search bracket is never drawn as a limit. If no root exists inside the bracket, the edge
  is absent and the envelope says "no deflection limit below the flute".
- **L5 (step 3): the engaged-edge force.** A method on P9's `Engagement` sums dF = Ks h + F_edge
  over the engaged arc with the helix lag (`tool/mod.rs` `instantaneous_flutes_in_cut`). It
  gives the mean and the peak resultant. The lateral force and the deflection read the PEAK
  engaged-edge force (deflection and breakage follow the peak). Power reads the mean (it
  already does through `PowerTerms`; check that the two agree in the mean, as step 0's closed
  form did within 1 %). The one-tooth peak model retires. Decide the open P9 diameters here:
  every reader uses `Engagement::d_eng` at the cut depth.
- **L6 (step 4): chip first.** `doc_derating_scale` stops scaling the chip. The vendor 1/2/3 x D
  table becomes a visible envelope limit "vendor depth ceiling (width not printed)", a `Warn`
  (Q6) when the operator point is past it. Step 0 shows the table does not hold force, chip or
  peak chip; do not derive it from the force model.
- **L7 (step 5): limits follow the feed.** The envelope is sized at the operator's chip (the
  stated feed / (rpm x z)), not at the suggested chip, so a higher chip moves the force,
  deflection and power edges in. A tool-strength (shank bending stress) limit lands only if a
  strength value for the tool material is printed in a repo source. If none is printed, add no
  constant; record it as a P12 reviewer item.
- **L8 (step 6): sources.** For each named constant (`SLOTTING_THRESHOLD`, `SLOTTING_DOC_CAP`,
  `adaptive_woc_factor`, `RUBBING_FLOOR_MM_TOOTH`, hardwood K_r, the deflection target): find a
  printed repo source or keep it an F201 Unsourced row; the report lists each. No new gate on an
  Unsourced constant beyond a warning (Q6).
- **L9: proof against the rules.** P11b ships an HTML page and an assert script in
  `research_p11_step0/after/` that run the shipped Suggest (CLI `suggest`) over the step 0 row
  set and state, per vendor family, where the Suggest chip and feed sit against the printed
  band. No pass rule is tuned after the run; the step 0 rule applies, and the page shows the
  result as it is. This is the input for the P12 reviewer.
