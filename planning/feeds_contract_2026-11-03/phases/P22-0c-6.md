# P22-0c-6: the feeds second definitions (survey and predictions)

Phase 6 of `P22-0c_DESIGN.md` (section 7): D3, D4, D5 and D6. Read-only
survey at HEAD 90e03440b, 2026-11-09. The tree also holds the uncommitted
P22-0c-5 edits (`tool/body.rs`, `tool/mod.rs` and others). Line numbers in
those two files can move when P22-0c-5 lands. No cargo job ran. Sections 0
to 2 are the survey agent's; the implementation agent adds the rest.

## 0. Summary

- D3 is smaller than the design says. Seven sites read "the shank, else a
  fallback", but in every feeds site the value goes to ONE place: the cap
  of the tapered-ball arm of `effective_diameter` (or of the axial point,
  where a flat end mill never reads it). So the feeds sites of D3 are D5
  sites. When D5 gives the hint door the cutting cap, those sites read no
  shank at all.
- After D5, a true "upper diameter" (the shank, else the envelope) has two
  readers only: the tool size label and the shank section of
  `tip_deflection_mm`. `ToolBody::upper_diameter_mm` does not exist yet.
- D5: the hint path caps a tapered ball at the collet shank; the cutter
  caps at the shaft. Operator ruling Q3 gives the shaft and says the cutting
  zone stops at the flute length. The operator's own example (R2 x 6 x 30.5,
  taper from the size) reaches the shaft at 32.47 mm, above the 30.5 mm
  flute. So "stop at the shaft" alone is not the ruling: the cap must be
  the width at the flute top. Neither path clamps at the flute top today.
- D6: `EngagedSection::AxialCircle` is a full circle. A drill cycle of a
  tool that does not cut at its centre cuts the ring. `A` and `J` both take
  the factor `1 - (d_core / D)^2`, so power and MRR both drop by it and the
  specific energy does not change.
- D4: `tip_deflection_mm` computes `(l - cutter_len).max(0.0)`, the same
  operands as `ToolBody::exposed_shank_length_mm`. The change is
  byte-identical. The other D4 readers already read the body.
- No existing test pins a number that this phase moves. No fixture has a
  tapered ball whose shank and shaft differ. No pin puts a depth above the
  flute top of a tapered ball. No pin has a drill cycle with a core. FM1:
  no cell moves. Wanaka: no row moves (tool 6 has no drill cycle; tool 4
  has shank = shaft and cuts at 0.2 mm).
- The proof of the changes therefore needs new sentries (section 2.4).

The ring, from the zone radii (`CuttingZone::inner_mm` = the core radius
`c`, outer `R = D / 2`):

- `A = pi (R^2 - c^2) = pi (D^2 - d_core^2) / 4`.
- `J = integral from c to R of 2 pi r dr = pi (D^2 - d_core^2) / 4`.
- Both terms of `P = [Ks A vf + F_edge Z n J] / 6e7` take the factor
  `k = 1 - (d_core / D)^2`. So `P_ring = k P_circle`, `MRR_ring = k MRR_circle`,
  and `u = P / MRR` does not change.

Not existing yet: a core input on the feeds side. `FeedsInput` has no core
field, and `ToolGeometryHint::Flat` has none. The section needs
`inner_diameter_mm` (0 for a centre-cutting tool), from
`body.core().map_or(0.0, CoreZone::diameter_mm)` (no `d / 2` outside the
body). `resolve_axial_point` has `&ToolConfig` only.

### 2.6 Open questions

1. Q3 scope: does the cutting width stop at the flute top, not only at the
   shaft? The operator's own example meets the shaft 2 mm above its flute.
   Recommendation: yes, the cap is `width(L)`, never above the shaft.
2. Apply that cap on the cutter path too (`of_tool`, `from_sample`, the
   simulation power, deflection and force gates), so the plan and the
   simulation give one width? It moves a number only for a sample deeper
   than `L` (the flute-overreach case). Recommendation: yes, in 6b.
3. The `FluteTop` contact zone has the envelope radius (shaft / 2). For a
   tapered ball whose cone does not reach the shaft at `L` (wanaka tool 4:
   2.905 mm, not 3.0) the true flute-top radius is `width(L) / 2`. This
   changes collision findings, so it is out of this phase.
   Recommendation: a later phase, with a count on `planning/fixtures/`.
4. How the hint-only callers (`operating_point.rs`, `adaptive_entry.rs`,
   `feeds_input_for_operation`) get the cap: (a) a `FeedsInput` field filled
   from the body (needs `build_cutter` in those paths; Suggest validates the
   tool first, so it cannot fail there — verify); (b) the shaft inside
   `ToolGeometryHint::TaperedBall` (self-contained like `VBit { tip_diameter }`,
   but 69 sites and it still lacks `L`); (c) a `ToolConfig` copy pinned
