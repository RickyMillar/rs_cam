core: P46: a left piece that the strip pass cut is not left; a rim place has its own reason; tests for three P45 hunks

Clean-up after P45 (decisions D-10, D-7 and D-4 of
planning/feeds_contract_2026-11-03/phases/P45_WALL_STRIP_PASS_2026-11-21.md).

What changes
- `level_contour::unleave_cut_pieces` (P45 D-10): after its rounds the strip
  pass removes a left piece of any level when a shipped cut of the pass lowered
  one of its cells (`StripTops::cut_at`) and none of its cells holds material
  above its level floor (`left_stock::piece_stands`). `clearing::unleave_pieces`
  is the inverse of `leave_piece`: the piece leaves `left_pieces` and its record
  leaves `unplanned`. A piece that the pass cut in part stays published. The
  P44b removal for the region contour is not changed.
- `left_stock`: the standing rule of a recorded piece is one function
  (`material_above_floor`).
- `PlaceWhy::LineMargin` (P45 D-4): a place of the finding
  `geom.rough_left_stock` that has a drop record and whose cells are all rim
  cells (`left_stock::rim_cell`: a strip cell nearer to a wall step than
  `contour_line_inset`) says "it is nearer to a wall than a contour line of the
  planner can run", not the reason of the record. A record only: no tool moves,
  and this rule changes no count.
- Tests (P45 D-7), each seen red with its hunk reverted alone
  (`p46/red.sh`, `p46/red_proofs.txt`): `hold_shipped_chords`, the centre test
  of the strip read of `wall_read`, the start of the ring of pass 3; the two
  rules of the piece removal; the rim reason. `tests::ContextFixture` builds a
  `ClearZLevelContext` for a test of a private planner function. No product
  code changed for testability.

Measured (release CLI; note
planning/feeds_contract_2026-11-03/phases/P46_CLEAN_UP_2026-11-22.md)
- Motion: rivmap100 keeps its bytes. The committed Wanaka job keeps each motion
  line of setup "1 Back" (the same bytes as the P45 build with id 41 off) and
  of setup "3 Front". 0 collisions.
- Id 2 "Back rest 6 mm": the pass reached 33 left pieces, 27 are removed; on 6
  the planner stock still reads material (a ray-top read) and they stay. Id 21 "Front 3D rough": 129, 25, 104.
- Finding of id 2: 349 to 323 named places (26 places lose their only record
  and join the sum with no record: 145 to 171); 6 of the 12 named places have
  the rim reason.

Not in this commit: the finding `project_curve_negative_depth` (the note,
section 3: its condition is false for "from below" on a setup that is not
flipped, and its auto-fix can cut the model; a decision for the lead).

BREAK: a 3D Rough with Geodesic Adaptive on remaining stock does not publish a
rest region for a piece that its strip pass cut to the floor, and the finding
`geom.rough_left_stock` does not count that piece. `PlaceWhy` has a new variant
`LineMargin`. No file format changes. No motion changes.
