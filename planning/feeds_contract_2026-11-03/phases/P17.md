### 5. Core: `tool_load/cut_force.rs` (new) and the deflection bound

What: the per-sample cut force through the one law, and the stock-to-leave deflection bound.

Where: `crates/rs_cam_core/src/tool_load/deflection.rs:108-148@ec5cbe4a9`

How, the force:

- `deflection::sample_engagement(tool, sample) -> Option<Engagement>` is factored out of
  `sample_tip_deflection_mm` (the `from_tool_cut` at `axial_engagement_mm` and the arc width
  `r (1 - cos psi)`, L5). `sample_tip_deflection_mm` and the new
  `cut_force::sample_peak_force_n(tool, material, sample, fz) -> Option<f64>` both read it;
  the force is `engagement.edge_force(line, fz, flutes)?.peak_resultant_n`
  (`feeds/engagement.rs:295@ec5cbe4a9`). No second engagement builder, no new constant.
  `tip_deflection_from_engagement` keeps its signature; its force is this force (deflection =
  compliance x force, one law).
- `cut_force::cut_points(ctx, env) -> Vec<CutPoint3>` walks the trace with the deflection
  gate's filters (cutting, not air, an arc, `is_steady_state_for_gate`) and the P16 feed door
  for `fz`.
- `cut_force::budget_line(ctx, env) -> Option<f64>`: `Some(budget_n)` only when
  `machine.rigidity.depth_cap_rule(family, role) == Some(DepthCapRule::ForceBudget(budget_n))`
  (ruling 3); `None` for a Factor rule, a finish pass, a drill, no machine.
- `DistributionMetric::CutForce`: `gate_population` collects `force_n` of `cut_points`;
  `metric_distribution` builds the histogram with `ceiling = budget_line`, `floor = None`,

  line and `None` (a reading) without it, `bound_source = Some(BoundSource::MachineForceBudget {
  budget_n, ae_mm, fz_mm, depth_mm })` of the peak sample under the line (the existing
  variant, `gates_export() == false`, so the card is advisory). A material with no force
  line: `NotMeasured(Unmodeled(MaterialUnvalidated))`; the card row reads "no force line".
- MCP and CLI parity: `ToolpathLoadVerdict::cut_force: Option<CutForceReading>` (serde
  default, skipped when `None`; report-only; `mark_simulation_stale` names it with `_`):
  `{ peak_n, peak_sample_index, population: GatePopulation, budget: Option<f64>, rule: &'static str }`
  built in `evaluate_population` from the same `cut_points`. `get_tool_load_report` serialises
  the verdict, so the agent reads the same peak and budget the card shows. `OwnMaterialVerdict`
  (S6) does not split it: the force reading is report-only and the S6 split names gates;
  recorded here as the reason.

How, the deflection bound (the derivation, for the record):

- The tip deflection of the deflection gate is the lateral displacement of the tool tip
  under the peak engaged-edge force. On a wall cut, the tip moves away from the wall by that
  displacement, so the wall is left proud by the same amount: the wall error equals the tip
  deflection (bending only, the gate's own assumption; the force acts normal to the wall at
  the peak of the arc).
- A rough pass leaves a radial stock to leave `s` on the wall for the finish pass to remove.
  A wall error up to `s` leaves material inside the finish allowance, which the finish pass
  cleans up. A wall error above `s` leaves material the finish pass does not reach, or (on
  the other side of the force) a wall cut below the finish surface. So the deflection limit
  of a rough pass is `s`: `limit_mm = radial_stock_to_leave_mm`.
- A finish pass has no stock to leave after it: the finish target
  `DEFLECTION_BACKOFF_TARGET_UM` (200 um, the F201 row) stays its limit.
- A rough op with no radial stock-to-leave field has no `s` to read: the 200 um target stays,
  and the confidence clause says "no stock-to-leave field on this op: the finish target
  applies".
- A rough op whose field is 0: the rough pass is the final wall, so the finish target
  applies, with the clause "stock to leave 0: the finish target applies". A limit of 0 would
  put every sample over it.
- Which rough configs have a radial leave at HEAD (`rg -n "pub stock_to_leave|pub finish_allowance" crates/rs_cam_core/src/compute/operation_configs.rs`):
  `BoreConfig::finish_allowance` (radial: "Radial stock the rough helix leaves on the wall")
  only. `Adaptive3dConfig::stock_to_leave_axial` is axial (Z alone, by its doc) and does not
  count. Every `stock_to_leave` field is on a finish op (Pencil, Scallop, UnifiedFinish,
  SteepShallow, RampFinish, SpiralFinish, RadialFinish, HorizontalFinish). So the rule bites
  on Bore today and on every rough op that gains a radial leave later; every other rough op
  takes the "no field" arm. The lead sees this; it is narrow by the data, not by the rule.
- The read: `OperationConfig::radial_stock_to_leave_mm() -> Option<f64>` (a new accessor
  beside `stock_to_leave_dial`, `compute/catalog.rs:1445@ec5cbe4a9`: `Bore(c) =>
  Some(c.finish_allowance)`, every other arm `None`, each named); `ToolpathLoadContext`
  gains `radial_stock_to_leave_mm: Option<f64>` and `load_context_parts` sets it. The 40
  struct-literal sites of `ToolpathLoadContext {` in tests gain one field (the lead's P16
  note 1 applies: a type change held by the compiler, about 30 fixture sites, is accepted).
- `DeflectionBounds` gains `basis: DeflectionBasis` (`FinishTarget | StockToLeave { mm }`);
  `as_criterion_status` maps `StockToLeave` to a new `BoundSource::StockToLeave { mm }`
  (`setting()` "operation", `gates_export()` true as `DeflectionBudget`, the clause "the
  radial stock to leave of this op, {mm:.2} mm: a tip that moves more than it cuts into the
  finish surface"). The F082 compliance door and the Suggest ceiling (200 um) are untouched:
  the envelope's Deflection limit line stays at the finish target on a rough pass (an open
  item below).

Callers: `rg -n "ToolpathLoadContext \{" crates` = 40; `rg -n "ToolpathLoadVerdict \{" crates`
