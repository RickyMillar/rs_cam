The rule (`adaptive3d/one_pass.rs`): a stock cell is too tall for a level when one tool centre
of the level, at its level floor, would take more than Depth/Pass plus the stay-down clearance
from it in one stamp (the covered part of the cell by the tool disc times the stock above the
cutter). Each material cell of the level grid that would take that much from such a cell is
not a region cell. `build_material_bool_grid` applies it, so Geodesic Adaptive, the Contour
Parallel rings and the clean-up raster read it, and the entries, links and order come from
the plan of the smaller region. `waterline_cleanup` masks its contour points with the same
read (`OnePass::blocks`).

  session adapter sets `RemainingStock` only when the op has a seed stock, beside the rest
