# P15: one `d_eng` door (the bull-nose defect, R30)

Status: IMPLEMENTED, NOT COMMITTED (implementer, 2026-11-06; lead reviews by path) | Contract: K7 | Ruling: operator 2026-11-06 (fix R30) | Numbers move: 6 FM1 cells (`power_kw`, bull nose, Trace); no G-OPGOLDEN row; no sim golden

Open for the lead: the V-bit tip term and the tapered-ball clamp (section "Residuals"); the calculator power ladder reads `d_eng` at the Step 5 depth, not at the candidate depth (section "Found, not changed").

## Goal
Every `Engagement` constructor reads one engaged diameter. A bull nose below its corner radius keeps
its flat centre: `d_eng = 2 sqrt(r^2 - (r - ap)^2) + (D - 2 r)`. The old hint rule
(`bull_nose_effective_diameter`) used the corner circle only: 6 mm r1 at ap 0.2 gave 1.2 mm, the
true value is 5.2 mm.

## Design
1. **One width function per shape (the F075 pattern).** `tool::ball_radius_at_height(R, h)` and
   `tool::bull_nose_radius_at_height(R, r, h)` are new free functions. `BallEndmill::width_at_height`
   and `BullNoseEndmill::width_at_height` call them, with the same float expressions as before (no
   toolpath bit moves). The `Ball` and `Bull` arms of `feeds::effective_diameter` call `2 x` the same
   functions. The Flat, V-bit and tapered-ball arms are not changed (the V-bit and tapered arms
   already call `tool::vbit_cone_radius_at_height` and `tool::ball_cone_radius_at_height`).
2. **One type at the door.** `feeds::engagement::EngagedDiameter` holds the diameter in a private
   field. It has two builders: `of_hint(hint, D, shank, ap)` = `effective_diameter`, and
   `of_tool(tool, ap)` = `2 x engagement_radius(max(ap, 0))`, never below 0. `Engagement::from_cut`
   takes an `EngagedDiameter`, not an `f64`, so no caller can pass a raw number; `from_tool_cut`
   calls `from_cut` with `of_tool`; `from_sample` reads `of_tool`. The compiler holds that each
   `d_eng` comes from one of the two builders. The shared width functions hold that the two builders
   agree for flat, ball and bull. The `_p15` sentry holds the dispatch.
3. **Why not route the built tool through the hint.** That makes one literal function, but it
   moves non-bull numbers (see "Residuals"). The lead's stop rule forbids that in P15.
4. **Deleted:** `feeds::geometry::ball_effective_diameter` and `bull_nose_effective_diameter`, and
   their unit tests. `rg` showed no other reader (one doc line in `session/mod.rs`, fixed).

## Ball at ap 0: the cutter's 0
The hint rule floored ap 0 at `0.01 x D` (and any value at 0.01 mm). The cutter gives 0. P15 takes 0.
Reason: the floor had no derivation (no source, no math), and a shared formula with a floor is a
second rule. Every reader of a zero `d_eng` already returns its degenerate answer:
- `immersion_angle` returns 0 for a zero radius; `peak_chip_thinning_lift` returns 1.
- `Engagement::edge_sums` returns `None` for `r <= 0` (no edge force, no deflection cap).
- `PowerTerms::of` drops the edge term for a zero diameter (`engagement_diameter_mm > 0` guard).
- `axial_chip_thinning_factor_for_ball` returns 1 for a zero effective diameter.
- The modulator thinning lift filters a non-positive `d_eng` (falls back to a unit diameter).
- `power_at_operating_point` returns `Err(NoEngagementDiameter)` for a ball at ap 0. Before, it gave
  a figure at a 0.06 mm circle. This is intended.
- The calculator never reaches ap 0 (`MIN_AP_MM`). No FM1 cell moved from this choice.

The ball arm now uses the cutter's expression `sqrt(2 R h - h^2)` in place of `sqrt(ap (D - ap))`.
P14 measured a 1 ulp difference at ap 0.2. No FM1 ball cell moved (the CSV prints 4 decimals).

## Residuals (need a lead ruling; P15 does not move them)
1. **Tipped V-bit.** The hint arm is `tip_d + 2 h tan(a/2)`; the cutter (`VBitEndmill::width_at_height`)
   is `2 h tan(a/2)` with no tip. `build_cutter` sets the cutter tip from `v_tip_planning_mm`, so a
   V-bit with a stated tip gives two values. P14 compared sharp V-bits only. A fix on the hint side
   moves Suggest for tipped V-bits; a fix on the cutter side moves toolpath geometry.
2. **Tapered-ball clamp.** The hint arm clamps at the `shank_d` its caller passes
   (`shank_diameter_planning_mm`); the cutter clamps at its own `shaft_diameter`. They agree only
   when the two fields are equal.

## Found, not changed
The calculator's power ladder (`calculator/power.rs`, `Ladder::required_at`) and Step 9's published
power (`calculator/close.rs`) read `Feed::effective_d`, the diameter at the Step 5 depth, also at a
lowered candidate depth. The type now makes this visible (one `EngagedDiameter` at one depth). A
fix moves numbers for every non-flat tool whose power ladder lowers the depth; it needs a ruling.
