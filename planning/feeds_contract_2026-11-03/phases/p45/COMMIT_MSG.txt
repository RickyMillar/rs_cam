core: P45: a 3D Rough on remaining stock ends with a strip pass that reads the stock at each cell centre

Operator ruling 2026-11-20: a 3D Rough cuts a place that it is told to cut, or
names the place and the reason. After P44b the wood that stayed where the tool
fits was mainly strips at walls: the level grid reads the stock at the
tool-centre cell only, so a strip narrower than the tool radius makes no region.

What changes
- `level_contour::strip_pass` (Geodesic Adaptive, remaining stock only,
  `OnePass::on`; `path.rs` calls it once after the last level): the standing
  cells of the bottom level with the strip read, a band under Min Region Cut
  Length recorded and not cut, each band grown on its open side
  (`grow_open_side`), then the region contour along each band in rounds, one
  Depth/Pass per round.
- `level_contour::StripTops`: the strip pass reads an upper bound of the stock
  at each cell centre, not the ray top. The stamp lowers a part-covered cell by
  a fraction, so the ray top of a cell at the edge of earlier discs is a blend.
  P44 build 2 failed for this reason (traced: 11 centres stood 3.2 to 7.8 mm
  above their ray top, 0.000 to 0.162 mm outside the disc edge of the level
  cuts; the strip pass cut them 6.61 to 8.50 mm deep at 6 mm per pass). The
  state starts at `DexelGrid::conservative_top` and goes down under each cut
  that the pass ships (`StripTops::scan`, along `shipped_cut_path`).
- `TriDexelStock::disc_holds_cell_core` (`stamping::point_holds_cell_core`):
  the disc holds a cell centre when the four inner sub-samples of the stamp are
  inside. The state and the read of the strip pass count a centre only with
  this test: a centre exactly on the disc edge is not cut.
- `level_contour::hold_shipped_chords`: a run of the strip pass is raised until
  each chord that the emitter ships holds the one-pass Z along it (the
  simplification of a run can drop a corner point of the line).
- `LeftWhy::NoLine`, `PlaceWhy::NoLine`: a band with no room for a contour line.
- The P42 wall pass, the P43 fresh wall pass and the P44b region contour keep
  their read (decision D-1 of the note).
