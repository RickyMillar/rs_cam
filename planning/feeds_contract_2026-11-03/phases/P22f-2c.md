What the two reads say:

- Both see the same kind of engagement: a layer of about 1 mm across the whole width (the floor
  drapes down 1 mm into the hollow, Z 16.5 to 15.5; every earlier pass at the 16.5 floor left
  it), and on one side a band of 3-4 strips (about 6 mm) standing 3-10 mm deep.
- The planner reads it by section: the area load is 0.06-0.36, about `c` (0.333). 1620 reads
  0.356 (capped, factor 0.935); 1621 reads 0.281 at most (uncapped, correctly by its rule).
- The simulation's gate width is `r (1 - cos psi)` at the arc: the thin layer spans the whole
  front, so the arc reads 2.9 rad and ae 17.7 mm. Its ap is the deepest cell (10 mm, the side
  band). The gate (and the P22a readout, and the P20i power figure) then judge a rectangle
  ap 10 x ae 17.7 (2.19 kW): two maxima of different parts of the cut. The sample's own removal
  ratio says the removed section is 4-12 % of that rectangle.

Not settled in two runs: the planner's strip section (about 36 strip-mm x 1.5 mm = 55 mm2 at
1621's worst sample) is larger than the simulation's mid-move removal (removal ratio 0.04-0.07
of 177 mm2, 7-12 mm2 per mm). The side band (strips -6 to -3) may lie at the disc edge, where the
planner counts a whole cell inside the disc and the simulation removes a sliver. A per-cell read
of that band (both stocks) answers it.

## 5. What a fix would be (one sentence; no design)

Judge a layered engagement by its section (the planner's area load, or the simulation's removal
per mm), not by the deepest cell times the widest arc: the P22a readout's width, the power gate's
`EngagedSection` and the P20i figures then read 1620/1621 near `c`, as the planner does.

## 6. Side finding

The removal ratio of the first and last sample of a move is 2-6 (the segment-time artefact at a
move end). The P22a readout and the P22f-2a sentry count a sample as real removal at a ratio of
0.5 or more, so their "real" counts include many move-end samples. The class means (for example
"uncapped draped 0.89") are lifted by them. A fix reads the ratio of mid-move samples only, or
the removal per mm over the move.
