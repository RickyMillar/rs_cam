## 1. What M4 is

The simulation folds the samples of a move that descends or climbs
(`dexel_stock/sample_coalesce.rs`). Before the fold each sample holds one depth (measured from
the code: `dexel_stock/simulation.rs`, `apply_subsegment_metrics`, writes one value to both
fields). After the fold a sample holds two depths:

- `axial_engagement_mm`: the MAXIMUM of the group. The depth gate, the deflection gate, the force
  reading and the deflection and force caps of the modulator read it.
- `axial_doc_mm`: the time MEAN of the group. `Engagement::from_sample` reads it, so the power
  gate, the chip lift, the cut of a sample, the K7 envelope placement and the power cap of the
  modulator read it.

So one sample gives two cuts. The maximum is never below the mean (measured on the operator file
and on rivmap100: 0 removing samples with an engaged depth below the mean depth).

## 2. What M4-A changes (planned)

1. `Engagement::from_sample` reads `axial_engagement_mm`. Its six callers then read the engaged
   depth (section 3).
2. The sample field `axial_doc_mm` keeps ONE reader in the product code: the per-move mean of the
   modulator (`session/compute.rs`). The field of that mean,
   `PerMoveEngagement::axial_doc_mm`, gets the name `mean_axial_mm`. The mean keeps its three
   uses: the zero test, the planner floor (`mean_binds`) and the fallback of a move with no sample
   engagement. No number of the mean moves.
3. The chip row key depth (`tool_load/groups.rs`, `tool_load/mod.rs`) reads the engaged depth.
   No number moves: `feeds::geometry::lut_key_diameter_for_cutter` discards its depth
   (`let _ = ap;`, finding F5).
4. `limits::SampleDepth`, the field `CutSource::Measured::depth`, `Caveat::SampleDepth` and
   `sample_depth_of` go. The exemption row `M4` goes, with its two `// EXEMPT(M4)` markers.
5. The pins of `the_exemption_list_only_shrinks_p29_3` go from 37 / 26 / 37 to 36 / 25 / 36
   (`PHYSICAL` 8 and `RULED` 3 do not move).

Not changed: the coalescer (M4-D is rejected), the sample field name `axial_doc_mm` (see section
5, decision 1), the summary field `peak_axial_doc_mm` (it reads the engaged depth already).

**Wire breaks (no alias):**

- The caveat `{"kind": "sample_depth", "depth": ..}` leaves every limit row (MCP
  `get_safe_envelope` `limit_rows`, the tool load report rows, the GUI Limits drawer detail). The
  detail text loses the clause "the sample depth read is the removed layer depth" or "... the
  engaged edge depth".
- The description of the MCP tool `get_safe_envelope` loses the word `sample_depth`. The snapshot
  of `mcp_wire_surface_pin` moves by that word.
- No key of a project file changes. No field of a sample changes.

## 3. Each caller and reader, and what it reads after the change

`Engagement::from_sample` (six callers):

| Caller | Before | After | A number moves? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Power gate (`tool_load/power.rs`, `sample_power_kw`) | mean depth | engaged depth | yes: the power of a folded sample rises |
| Chip lift (`tool_load/display.rs`, `sample_thinning_lift`) | mean depth | engaged depth | no: the lift reads `ae / d_eng = (1 - cos psi) / 2`, which holds no depth (design 3.4; a difference in the last bit is possible) |
| Peak chip (`tool_load/display.rs`) | mean depth | engaged depth | no, the same reason |
| Cut of a sample (`limits/sample.rs`, `Cut::of_sample`) | mean depth | engaged depth | the engagement of the cut record |
| Modulator power cap (`dressup/feed_modulation.rs`, `SampleEngagement::of_sample`) | mean depth | engaged depth | **yes: the cap falls on a folded sample** |
| K7 envelope placement (`compute/simulate.rs`, `envelope_placement`) | mean depth | engaged depth | yes: a folded body sample is placed deeper; `project.sim_outside_envelope` can count more samples |

Readers of `axial_doc_mm` (the sample field):

| Reader | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Per-move mean of the modulator (`session/compute.rs`) | `axial_doc_mm` | `axial_doc_mm` (the one reader); the field of the mean is `PerMoveEngagement::mean_axial_mm` |
| Chip row key depth (`tool_load/groups.rs`, `tool_load/mod.rs`) | `axial_doc_mm` | `axial_engagement_mm`; no number reads the key depth |

| The sample on the wire (serde field `axial_doc_mm`) | the mean | the mean; the doc comment says so |

`SampleDepth` sites: `limits/cut.rs` (the enum, the field of `CutSource::Measured`),
`limits/sample.rs`, `limits/row.rs` (the caveat, its match arms, `sample_depth_of`),
`limits/mod.rs`, `limits/exemptions.rs` (row `M4`), the tests

## 4. Predictions (before the code)

### 4.1 The instrument

The probe `zz_p31_probe_m4` (a core test, not kept; source in the scratchpad) runs the generation
plan of core on a project file with feed modulation in the mode `M4_MODE`, and writes for each
move the emitted feed, the dressed feed and the binding cap; for each op the cycle time, the limit
rows, the envelope placement, the diagnostics and the held moves; and the export warnings.

Proof of the probe (measured): two runs on `planning/fixtures/rivmap100/rivmap100.toml` in cap
only give byte-equal files (218 616 moves). Three runs on the operator file in cap only and two in
full give byte-equal CSV files (1 crossed check each), so a difference after the code is the code.

The baseline is the operator file `planning/rivmap_block_job/nz_wanaka_21_operator.toml`
(sha256 `ee6869be015b633b...`, read only), the WHOLE project (20 plan steps, 18 ops, 0 refused),
cell 0.2 mm, release build of `b9c2fe66e`. Peak memory 16.8 GB, about 5 minutes for each run.

Two prediction dumps ran on the baseline tree, with no product edit:

- **P-cap**: for each removing sample, the power of a planned cut at the ENGAGED depth and the
  measured arc (`Engagement::from_tool_cut`), at two feeds. Power is affine in the feed, so two
  points give the feed at which the sample meets the available power: the power cap of M4-A. The
  cap of a move is the minimum over its samples.
- **P-A**: the limit rows, the envelope placement, the diagnostics and the export warnings on a
  copy of the trace with `axial_doc_mm := axial_engagement_mm` (the trace "A" of design 3.3). It
  reads the feeds of the baseline run, so it shows the rows BEFORE the modulator lowers a feed.

## 5. Decisions for the lead (before the code)

1. **The rename.** The design says "`axial_doc_mm` keeps one reader, the modulator's per-move mean
   (renamed `mean_axial_mm`)". No note says which field takes the name. Built: the field of the
   mean, `PerMoveEngagement::axial_doc_mm`, becomes `mean_axial_mm`. The sample field keeps the
   name `axial_doc_mm`, because a rename of it touches about 110 files and the MCP sample wire.
   The other reading (rename the sample field) is open for the clean-up pass.
2. **The chip row key depth** reads the engaged depth (section 2, item 3). The design does not
   name it; no number reads it.
3. **The probe grew** against the P31 version: the mode, the dressed feed, the rows, the
   placement, the warnings, the depth statistics and the two prediction dumps. The baseline CSV
   files come from the first build; the prediction dumps come from two later builds of the same
   product tree (their CSV files are byte-equal to the baseline).
