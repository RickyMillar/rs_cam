planning: P44 record: a 3D Rough that cuts each place where its tool fits (not landed); the cause per place, two measured builds, the patch

The operator ruled on 2026-11-20 that a 3D Rough must cut a place that it is told to cut, or
name the place and the reason. P44 measured the cause of each of the seven places of the
Wanaka back, wrote a design and measured two builds. The product code is NOT changed: bar 2
fails, so the tree is at HEAD and the patch is in phases/p44/not_landed/.

The cause (id 2 "Back rest 6 mm", 9 193.9 mm2 of wood where the 6 mm tool fits):
- the small-region bound of `detect_and_order_regions` (144 mm2, an area in the material
  frame applied to a tool-centre region): 1 782.6 mm2, and 1 053.3 mm2 more that the one-pass
  hold then keeps (the antenna slot, the control relief);
- the F-038 forecast, which insets a tool-centre region by the tool radius again: 2 827.7 mm2
  (the slit slot reads 0.0 mm against 15 mm);
- strips at the walls that make no region, because the level grid reads the stock only at
  the centre cell: 3 430.8 mm2.

412.8 to 59.8, control relief 158.2 to 13.6, slit slot 1 781.7 to 121.8, ESP relief 59.4 to
23.8, step shelf 795.1 to 124.6, board pocket 957.4 to 364.2, cavity 5 016.1 to 762.0); id 2

"3 Front" 8 762.4 to 9 183.4 s; 0 collisions; the depth rows of ids 1 and 2 hold.

Build 3 (no strip pass): wood 9 193.9 to 4 463.2 mm2, id 2 579.7 to 676.9 s of feed time, the
replay class 0 pieces. It is a measure form, not a product form.

Why build 2 is not landed: the corrected replay class of id 2 has 4 pieces (11 nodes, deepest
8.50 mm, at most 0.199 mm inside the disc edge); HEAD has 0. Places 1 to 4 keep the rim of the
plan margin (0.2 to 0.4 mm at each wall), so they are not under 10 mm2.
