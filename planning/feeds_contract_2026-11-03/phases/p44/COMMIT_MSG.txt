core: P44: a 3D Rough plans each region where its tool fits; the cut-length dial is the one drop rule; a slot with no room for the field is cut on remaining stock

The operator ruled on 2026-11-20 that a 3D Rough cuts a place that it is told to cut, or
names the place and the reason. This is the product form of build 3 of P44 (lead rulings of
2026-11-21): no strip pass, no env switch.

What changes:
- H1. `detect_and_order_regions` has no small-region bound. The bound was
  `(2 D)^2 x min(DPP / R, 1)` with a floor of `D^2 / 4`: an area in the material frame, with
  no derivation, on a region that is a set of tool centres. `LeftWhy::SmallRegion` is deleted.
- H2. The F-038 forecast reads a region in its own frame: the length of its contour line
  (`level_contour::contour_line_inset`, one planner cell plus the cut tolerance inside the raw
  outline). It moved the outline inside by the tool radius plus 0.25 mm before, a second inset
  by the tool radius, and read a slot region of any length as 0.
- H3. `level_contour::region_contour_pass`: on remaining stock (`OnePass::on`) a region whose
  plan ships no cut (no room for the geodesic field) gets one pass along its contour line,
  with the reads, the rounds, the one-pass guard, the load cap and the emission of the P42
  wall pass (`contour_rounds`, `ContourRule::Region`). On a fresh block such a region stays
  (a coarse planner cell cannot read a sliver beside the line: 10.3 mm deep in build 1), and
  the finding names it.
- H5. `standing.rs` reads the stock that stands where the tool fits at the end of the plan.
  `geom.rough_left_stock` lists each place that has a drop record (position, area, height,
  reason; the 12 largest) and gives one count and one area for the places with no record.
  A place with no record does not raise the finding alone (`LeftStock::stands`).
- A left piece is in tool-centre space at every `leave_piece` call (D48, lead ruling D-15):
  the no-field path records the part of the region inside the planner outline, not the
  planner outline. On remaining stock a region that the region contour cut is removed from
  `left_pieces` and from the drop record: it is not left.
- A cut group that the F-038 group filter drops gets a drop record with the box of its cut
  (`LeftWhy::ShortGroup`, lead ruling D-11). The finding names such a place by that reason
  (`PlaceWhy::ShortGroup`). The record moves no tool and is not a left piece.
- The strip pass of builds 1 and 2 is NOT in this commit (4 replay pieces at the disc edge).
  The patches stay in planning/feeds_contract_2026-11-03/phases/p44/not_landed/.
