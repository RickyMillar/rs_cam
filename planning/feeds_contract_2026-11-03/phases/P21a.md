## 2. The design of the door

- `calculator/spindle.rs::rpm_choice(input, chip, speed, log) -> Option<RpmChoice>` holds the
  bodies of Steps 2b and 2b' in their old order, with the same arithmetic, the same basis and the
  same two step records (`step2b_max_speed`, `step2b_milling_tier`). `speed.rs` loses the two
  functions.
- `RpmChoice { rpm, binding: RpmLimit, limits: Vec<RpmLimitValue> }`. `rpm` is the RPM after
  Step 2b'. Each `RpmLimitValue { limit, rpm }` is the highest RPM that limit admits.
- `RpmLimit` members in P21a: `LegacySpeedup`, `LegacyHeadroom`, `LegacyTier`, `VendorRpmMax`.
  No wildcard arm.
- The legacy rule only lifts the RPM, except the tier on a milling op:
  - `LegacySpeedup` admits the chart RPM times `MAX_SPINDLE_SPEEDUP`.
  - `LegacyHeadroom` and `VendorRpmMax` admit the larger of their cap and the chart RPM (a cap
    under the chart RPM does not lower it).
  - `LegacyTier` admits the tier on a milling op with a nominal-diameter tool (Step 2b' lowers to
    it). On a drill it admits the larger of the tier and the chart RPM (Step 2b only).
  - A V-bit and a tapered ball have no `LegacyTier`.
  - `VendorRpmMax` is present only when the row prints a finite positive `rpm_max`.
- The machine clamp `MachineProfile::clamp_rpm` applies after the minimum. On a variable spindle
  inside its range the two are equal. On a discrete spindle the clamp snaps the RPM, so `rpm` can
  differ from the minimum.
- Steps 6 and 7 can lower the RPM after the choice (`PowerLimit`, `FeedCeiling`). P21a does not
  name them; P21c does.
- `FeedsResult::rpm_choice` is `Some` under `MaxSpeed` and `None` under `MatchChart`.

### 4.1 What the sentry reads

The grid is every flat pocket and flat 2D adaptive cell of `cells.toml` that the shim runs:
22 cells, at 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 6, 10 and 12 mm. Cells with no Suggest basis (for example
`flat_1p5mm_adaptive2d_oak`) refuse in the shim and are not in the grid.

- `LegacyTier` binds on 17 cells, `LegacySpeedup` on 2 (the 12 mm adaptive cells, chart RPM 8000),
  `VendorRpmMax` on 3 (al6061, fiberglass, and the hdpe cell, where it ties with the tier at 20000).
  `LegacyHeadroom` binds on no cell: on this machine it is above every tier.
- Step 7 (`FeedCeiling`) lowers the shipped RPM below the choice on 15 of the 22 cells. The
  sentry asserts that the shipped RPM is below the choice there, and equal to it on the others.
  P21c makes the feed ceiling a named limit.
