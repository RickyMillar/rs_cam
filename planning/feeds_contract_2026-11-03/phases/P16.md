### 1. What "matched" means

A member is a vendor row that passes every rule below for the query:
1. The lookup's existing hard filters (tool family compatible, material category).
2. The same material family as the query, after the one hardness reading
   (`row_and_query_hardness` reads a missing hardness as the generic species of the family, in
   the transfer and in the score: the D2 first half).
3. The same operation family and pass role as the winner, after the existing routes
   (`vendor_normalize::lut_query_for`, `LutRoute::FlatAsRoughPocket`, the G3 family rules).
4. The size: a printed row at the query size (G1 `Exact`), or a form A interpolation between
   two printed sizes of one chart series (one member per series, at the interpolated value).
   Forms B and C (extrapolation) give no member. When no member exists, the cell keeps today's
   single-row rule (the band is the winner alone).
5. The flute count: the same printed count, or a chart that prints no count (Freud).
6. The source prints a chip: a row of the hobby class never enters. The hobby class is a
   typed field on the source record (`source_manifest.json` and the loaded `VendorObservation`
   source class), not a list in code. Candidates in the LUT today: `idcwoodcraft_millmage.json`
   (`idcwoodcraft_chipload_csv_2026-05-30`, grade c, a community CSV of the IDC hobby chart)
   and `whiteside_fusion360.json` (`whiteside_fusion360_tools_2019-10-23`, a community Fusion
   360 library). Lead to confirm each.

Each member carries its chip after its own size and hardness scales (the band each consumer
reads today for that row).

### 2. Where the band lives

- Core: the lookup result. `LookupResult::tied_with: Option<RowRef>` (the Q3 tie) becomes the
