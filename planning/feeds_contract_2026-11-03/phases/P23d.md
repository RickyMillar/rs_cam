# P23d: the Pocket feeds its slot-first ring at the unlifted feed (2026-11-10)

Scope: the ruling in `P23d_survey.md` section 5 (operator "yes to the lower feed"; the table row
grades the slot-ring feed). Pocket, Contour pattern, only. Base `2d0f7849f`. No commit, no bless.
Section 1 is written before the code.

## 1. Predictions

### 1.1 The rule

- The first ring of each piece of each level (the compensated boundary, `ops/pocket.rs`: "the
  outermost cutting contour") is the slot-first ring. Its cutting moves take the unlifted feed:
  the op feed over the lift that Suggest applied (`geometry::UnliftedFeed`), and
  `Move::feed_cap` holds that value. The plunge, the rapids and every later ring do not change.
- A Pocket whose lift is 1 (stepover at or above half the diameter) does not change at all: no
  lower feed, no cap, byte-identical output.
- One function gives the value to the generator and to Suggest
  (`SuggestedSpeeds::slot_ring_feed_mm_min`), so the card value is the cut value.

### 1.2 wanaka idx 6

floor of the op feed; the difference is the floor, 0.03 %). Later rings: 1347.

### 1.4 Open items (not coded)

- Zigzag (and the Zigzag pattern of Pocket): the first raster line of each level is a slot. It
  runs at the lifted op feed today.
- 2D Adaptive with `slot_clearing`: the opt-in slot lines run at the op feed (lifted at `c D`
  since P23a) and are exempt from the pass ceiling.
- An island's first ring (`comp.holes` of the compensated boundary, emitted right after the wall
  ring): the stock around an island is fresh when it is cut unless the wall slot overlaps it. The
  generator does not know which without the stock. It runs at the lifted op feed today.
- A rest Pocket (remaining stock): its first ring is not a slot by construction. P23d feeds it
  at the unlifted feed too (the lower feed is the safe side); a stock-aware rule can lift it.
- The entry moves of the slot ring: the entry dressup turns the plunge into a ramp along the
  ring at the entry feed (the op feed on the fixture). The cap is on the cut alone; the ramp
  feed has its own rule (G6, G10).
- The post writes a feed as a whole mm/min, to the nearest: the capped 942.8 prints `F943`.

## 2. The shape that landed

- `feeds::geometry::UnliftedFeed::of_slot_ring(operation, hint, D, cutting cap)`: the one door.
  `Some` for a contour Pocket with a feed; the op feed over the lift of its stepover
  (`PowerCases::full_feed_width`, the Step 5 width) on the engaged diameter at its depth.
- `ops/pocket.rs`: `SlotFirstRings` and `pocket_contours_to_sided_toolpath_slot_first`. The old
  kernel is the same function with no slot ring. The adapter
  (`compute/execute/clearing_2d.rs`, `PocketContour`) records the index of the first ring of each
  piece (`PocketRings::first_rings`) and passes the feed only when it is below the op feed.
- The provenance of the cap: `Move::feed_cap` alone. No named 2D source exists;
  `FeedCapSource` and its events are `adaptive3d/` types, which P23d does not touch. The value
  and the rule are stated by `of_slot_ring` and by `SuggestedSpeeds::slot_ring_feed_mm_min`.
- `feeds::profile::SuggestedSpeeds::slot_ring_feed_mm_min` (`None` for an op with no slot-first
  ring); MCP `basis.rpm.slot_ring_feed_mm_min`; the CLI prints the struct.

### 3.1 The 18 new G-OPGOLDEN changes

Compared key by key with `opgolden_known_46_rows.txt`: every known key is present with its known
value; 18 keys are new.

- **10 as predicted:** `pocket/each_face`, `pocket/each_face_flipped`, `pocket/extend_open`,
  `pocket/fresh`, `pocket/rest`: `moves_hash` and `gcode_hash` each. No count, length, bbox or
  span field of a Pocket row moves. `pocket/zigzag` does not move.
- **8 not predicted, `gcode_hash` only:** `adaptive/rest`, `bore/rest`, `chamfer/rest`,
  `drill/rest`, `face/rest`, `profile/rest`, `trace/rest`, `zigzag/rest`. Cause: the rest arm of
  the flat fixture runs a prior contour Pocket (`tests/common/op_fixtures.rs::flat_prior_op`),
  and `gcode_hash` is the hash of the exported program, which holds the prior op. The case op's
  own `moves_hash`, counts and lengths do not move on those rows. So no geometry moves and no
  non-Pocket toolpath moves; eight non-Pocket ROWS move through the Pocket in their program.
  This meets the lead's stop condition "a non-Pocket row moves" by the letter: reported, not
  blessed.
