core: P42 step 0: a depth row states the deepest sample of its group beside the judged sample (`DeepestSample`, wire `limit_rows[..].deepest`)

The depth row of a simulated toolpath prints the judged sample: the
largest depth over its own force-budget cap (P29-6 6c, ruling Q3). It is
not the deepest sample. Measured on the Wanaka back (P41 R7, run again):
the body row of "Back rest 6 mm" read 6.0 mm while two samples of the same
group read `axial_engagement_mm` 8.00.

The cause. No filter drops the two samples: both are clearing cuts, not in
a transit span, and both have an arc. A load cap of the planner slowed them
to 1 623 and 2 068 mm/min (stated feed 5 279), so their chip is about a
third of the chip of the judged sample, their own cap is deeper, and 8.00
over that cap is a smaller ratio than 6.00 over 3.197. A sample with no arc
has no number at all. Both rules are correct for a force judgement. The
defect: the deepest depth was in the detail sentence only, and a reader
took `value` as the deepest cut.

The change.
- `tool_load::verdict::DeepestSample { depth_mm, sample_index }`: the
  largest `axial_engagement_mm` of every sample of the group that removes
  material at a depth, with or without a width.
- The depth gate puts it on `SampleEvidence::deepest` of each verdict with
  evidence; `CriterionStatus::deepest` carries it; `limits::CheckRow` has
  the field `deepest` on the wire (absent on a row with none).
- `value`, `bound`, `margin`, the state, the face and the detail of a row
  do not change. No gate outcome changes.

Tests: `--lib the_verdict_states_the_deepest_sample_beside_the_judged_one_p42`
(red when the gate does not fill the record) and
`--lib a_depth_row_states_the_deepest_sample_p42` (the wire key).
`phases/p42/lim.py` prints the number.

Not done: no surface shows the number in its row face. The GUI drawer and
the CLI summary print the detail sentence, which names the deepest depth as
before.
