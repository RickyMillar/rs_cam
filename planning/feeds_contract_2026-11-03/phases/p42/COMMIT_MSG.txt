planning: P42: the wall pass of each level of a rest rough: the cause, the design, the measures, and the patch that is not landed

sample on a depth row) is its own commit.

its tool off stock taller than one pass. It does not cut that stock, and
under it each lower level stands 0.4 to 0.7 mm further from a wall (measured
at the wall Y 104 of the Wanaka back).

cell inside its raw outline, where stock stands on cells that no centre of
the level can plan (`not_landed/w6_wall_pass.patch`, with its own commit
message and gate results). Measured, HEAD against the patch:

  and slit pockets 1 631.5 to 964.0 mm2; the slit V-bit 10.60 to 7.44 mm of
  wood over its tip; 0 collisions; no new red in the gates.

  target raster; with one node lowered 0.1 against 0.2).
