P41 is a guard: a level keeps its tool off stock that stands more than one

that stock. Measured on the Wanaka back: the plan of each level keeps its

The wall pass (`adaptive3d/level_contour.rs`). After the plan of a level,
one contour runs along the boundary of each region that the plan cut, where
stock stands in the tool disc on cells where no centre of the level has
material to plan (`wall_read`).
- The line: the raw outline of the region, one planner cell plus the cut
  tolerance inside (the least distance of a centre from the raw boundary,
  `grown_outlines` D19), simplified and sampled as the C4 band line.
- The ship rule (`wall_point_ships`, lead rulings D-1): a point ships only
  at a wall deeper than one pass. Either the next level would meet the
  strip more than the one-pass limit above its cutter
  (`OnePass::over_limit` against the level floor at `LevelSlot::next_z`),
  or the model wall top over the strip stands more than the limit above the
  floor of this level. A wall deeper than one pass gets the pass at each
  level that reaches it; a stair tread of a draped slope gets none.
- The Z: the level floor, raised so that no cell whose centre is inside the
  disc stands more than one Depth/Pass above the cutter. The read repeats
  while a round lowers the strip.
- Not cut: a point outside the boundary clip, a point that the one-pass
  rule blocks, and a point within one planner cell of held stock taller
  than the limit.
- The runs, the gap merge, the load cap (`FeedCapStart`,
  `FeedCapSource::LevelContour`) and the emission are those of the C4
  contour: `run_feed_cap` and `emit_runs` are shared, so the planner stamps
  the path that the emitter ships.
- No dial of its own (ruling D-4): the pass is part of
  `OnePassHold::RemainingStock`, which now carries the link timing of the
  gap merge. A fresh rough and a Contour Parallel rough do not get it.
- `OnePass::over_limit` is the one height test of the rule; `deep_cell`
  reads it too.
