planning: P42 round 2: the wall pass with the next-level rule fails two bars; the patch and the numbers

A record. No code of the wall pass is in the tree.

Round 2 applied the lead rulings to the round 1 patch:
- D-1: a wall point of level N ships only where the strip top is more than
  the one-pass limit above the cutter of the next level
  (`OnePass::over_limit` against the level floor at the next level; the
  last level: its own floor). The slope rule is removed.
- D-10: measured first. The wall cleanup row exceeded because of the WIDTH
  of the cut (4.27 mm at the judged sample), not the feed. A strip up to
  about 1.1 mm wide is within the force budget at one pass and the stated
  feed. The existing cap law holds the chip-area load, and the planner has
  no force model, so a feed cap by that law cannot hold the row. The trial
  built a width rule in its place: the pass cuts only the margin strip of
  the plan (0.85 mm, the three margin numbers of the plan).
- D-4: no dial; the pass is part of `OnePassHold::RemainingStock`.

Measured (note P42, section 10.4), HEAD against round 2:
- "Front 3D rough": the HEAD G-code, byte for byte (868 s). D-1 does what
  the ruling expects on terrain.
- "Back rest 6 mm": body and deepest sample 6.0 mm, entry 1.89 mm, 0 replay
  pieces deeper than 6.3 mm, the slit V-bit 7.44 mm, one node below the
  target 0.1 mm2, 0 collisions, the fresh rough the same section.
- FAILS: the wall cleanup row still exceeds (6.0 mm against 4.06 mm: the
  simulation reads a sample 3.10 mm wide where the planner read under
  0.85 mm).
- FAILS: wood in the mech and slit pockets 1 387.5 mm2 (bar under 1 155.6;
  HEAD 1 631.5; the round 1 patch 964.0). The last level of a wall ships no
  pass, and a wide place ships none.
- id 2 takes 633 s (522 at HEAD): 166 ramp entries.

Open: the row stays within only with the force model in the planner for the
cap of a wall run, or with a ruling that the row may exceed (advisory, as
the body row does at HEAD).
