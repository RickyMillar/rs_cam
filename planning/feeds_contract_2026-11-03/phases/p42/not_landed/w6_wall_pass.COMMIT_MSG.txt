The patch is NOT in the tree (note P42, section 0). Apply: `git apply <patch>` on a tree that
holds HEAD `03762ea4e` or HEAD plus step 0.

core: P42: each level of a Geodesic rough on remaining stock cuts to the wall that it can reach (`region_wall_pass`); the wood in the mech and slit pockets of the wanaka back falls from 1 631.5 to 964.0 mm2

P41 is a guard: a level keeps its tool off stock that stands more than one
Depth/Pass plus the stay-down clearance above the cutter. It does not cut
that stock. Measured on the Wanaka back: the plan of each level keeps its
centre 0.65 mm from the tool-centre limit of a wall (the margin of the
plan), so a strip stands from the stock top; under the guard each lower
level stands 0.4 to 0.7 mm further off, because the guard removes the
centres that meet the strip and the plan keeps its margin again.

The wall pass. After the plan of a level, one contour runs along the
boundary of each region that the plan cut, where stock stands in the tool
disc on cells where no centre of the level has material to plan
(`level_contour::region_wall_pass`, `wall_read`).
- The line: the raw outline of the region, one planner cell plus the cut
  tolerance inside (the least distance of a centre from the raw boundary,
  `grown_outlines` D19), simplified and sampled as the C4 band line.
- The Z: the level floor, raised so that no cell whose centre is inside the
  disc stands more than one Depth/Pass above the cutter. The read repeats
  while a round lowers the strip, so stock taller than one pass goes down in
  passes.
- Not cut: a point outside the boundary clip, a point that the one-pass
  rule blocks, a point within one planner cell of held stock taller than
  the limit, and a point with no wall in its disc (the slope rule of the
  wall cleanup, now one constant `WALL_STEEP_ANGLE_RAD`).
- The runs, the gap merge, the load cap (`FeedCapStart`,
  `FeedCapSource::LevelContour`) and the emission are those of the C4
  contour: `run_feed_cap` and `emit_runs` are shared, so the planner stamps
  the path that the emitter ships.
- The switch is the dial of P41: `OnePassHold::RemainingStock` now carries
  the link timing of the gap merge. A fresh rough and a Contour Parallel
  rough do not get the pass.
