# P3: Every op states how it enters material (`EntryContract`, today's behaviour)

Status: NOT STARTED | Contracts: K4 | Rulings: Q6 (warn, no block), Q8 (recorded, not acted on here) | Numbers move: no. Move intents and span labels change; G-code bytes do not.

## Goal

After this phase, each of the 25 registry rows holds one required `EntryContract`. The contract
states today's entry behaviour of the op. `DressupConfig::for_op`, `normalize_for_op` and the
dressup pipeline read the contract, not `EntryStylePolicy`. The air lead above an entry has its
own intent, `MoveIntent::EntryApproach`. `Produced` carries one `EntryRecord` per entry. The
emitted G-code of every op stays byte-identical.

     /// what the compiler refuses in its doc (see `entry_contract_refusals`).
     #[derive(Debug, Clone, Copy, PartialEq, Eq)]
     pub enum EntryContract {
         /// The dressup builds the entry. `allowed` is non-empty.
         Dressup { allowed: EntrySet, default: RoleDefault, none_is: NoneEntry, own: Option<EntrySet> },
         /// The planner builds its own entries (3D Rough, Bore, Unified Finish).
