## 1. Predictions (written before the wanaka run)

- The wide samples (measured width over the pass ceiling `c D`) come from four sources: the
  departure windows out of the modelled plunge (the 3D arm sets `entry_helix_radius: 0.0`, so
  every entry is a plunge in the planner model, `P22_DESIGN.md` section 1), the D65 2D capped

  D65 3D split. The move index at this HEAD differs from the P20 index (P22-0a..0c changed the

- The departure windows give the second largest count. The corridor case of P22e ("no field"
  pieces) gives no wide sample, because those pieces go to the rest op (`leave_piece`).

## 2. The power agreement is closed (P20i, commit c385fc53b)

The second half of P22a in `P22_DESIGN.md` section 6 (Suggest, `get_safe_envelope` and the power
gate give one kW for one engagement) is closed. P20i (`phases/P20i.md` section 2.2 and 5.2,
commit `c385fc53b`) measured the cause: the "Ks 93.6, F_edge 5.91" fit read the grid row at the
nominal `ae`, but the grid power is the `PlannerCap` case at the width `min(1.6 ae, D)`. The two
ratios (1.5996 and 1.2862) agree with the case width to 4 digits. Grid, Suggest and the gate
agree within 1e-9 at one engagement. No second law, no derate and no RPM difference exist. This
phase does no more work on it. The P22 power predictions that use the fit must use the law at
the true width.

## 3. Design: the doors the readout reads

Test file: `crates/rs_cam_core/tests/every_wide_step_has_a_named_source_p22a.rs`.

| Quantity | Door (public) |
|---|---|
| Width of a sample | `feeds::engagement::Engagement::from_sample(sample, tool).ae_mm()`: the power gate's width, `r (1 - cos psi)` at the measured arc |
| Pass ceiling `c` | `ops::adaptive_shared::radial_woc_fraction_from_leading_arc(adaptive::pass_engagement_limit(s, R))`: the value the 3D stock-read split reads; `pass_engagement_limit` holds `PASS_ENGAGEMENT_TOLERANCE` (0.05) |
| Phantom samples | `tool_load::locality::is_phantom_transit` with `SpanLookup` on the result's spans (the gates' own filter) |
| Entry samples | `Move::intent` (`EntryRamp`, `EntryHelix`, `EntryPlunge`) |
| Planner cap | `Move::feed_cap` (written only by the 3D emitter between `FeedCapStart` and `FeedCapEnd`) |
| Planner rule constants | read from the source text (comments stripped): `PLUNGE_SLOPE_LIMIT`, `Z_DROP_FLOOR_MM` and the `depth_per_pass` factor (`adaptive3d/clearing.rs`), `PLANNER_CELLS_PER_TOOL_RADIUS` (`adaptive/path.rs`) |

No public door names the source of a cap. `geodesic::slice::feed_caps` returns only the factor;
`Adaptive3dRuntimeEvent::FeedCapStart { factor }` has no source; the emitter
(`adaptive3d/path.rs` ~2256) consumes the marker and writes no annotation. Thus three classes are
INFERRED from the planner rule that writes each one:

| Class | Rule (on the emitted moves) |
|---|---|
| departure window (inferred) | a capped run (consecutive clearing moves with one `feed_cap`) in the first cut after an entry whose start lies within R (XY) of the entry's end; the 2D rule of `fed_runs` |
| D65 3D split (inferred) | another capped run whose net step meets the shape-split rule (`|dz| > max(1.1 dpp, 1)` or `|dz| / xy > 0.3`); only a shape-split step can be a capped split (`admits_capped_split`) |
| 2D cap <= turn bound (inferred) | another capped run no longer (XY) than `turn_bound = s + (1 + sqrt 2) cell`: a planned turn or a short D65 2D window |
| D65 2D > turn bound (inferred) | a capped run longer than the turn bound: not one turn |

Exact classes: phantom transit, ramp / helix / plunge entry, keep-down link, the joint class (a
sample of an uncapped flat clearing move within one planner cell plus one sim cell of the end it
shares with a capped run or a draped step), the uncapped draped step (an uncapped clearing move
with a Z change), and OTHER (an uncapped flat clearing move, and any other intent).

Columns: samples over `c D`; over `c D` plus one sim cell; over `c D` plus one planner cell plus
one sim cell (the read precision of both lattices; `adaptive/CLAUDE.md`: the 2D rule admits a
sliver to ceiling + cell / D, and the sim blends partial cells).

The fixture arm asserts on the last column. The simulation runs with no feed modulation, so
`feed_cap` is the planner's cap.

## 6. What the readout says for the next phases

- **The first fix is not in P22b-P22e.** The perimeter sweep is the main wide source on idx 1:
  a full slot at full feed, with no cap and no planner load read. A new phase (proposed name
  P22f, before P22b) should make the geodesic slice never cut the sweep: find why
  `grown_outlines` returns `None` on these three regions, and either fix the grow or, where no
  grow exists, plan the band under the pass load (the geodesic field on the raw region), or at
  least cap the sweep's feed by `c / L` as D65 does. The P20g worst-sample modulator already
  lowers the sweep's feed after the simulation; the planner should not ask for it.
- **P22b (helix bore)**: the departure windows are real (79 samples, ap 9, up to 14.2 mm) and
  capped; P22b removes them as designed. The ramp-entry samples (2066) are at a small ap at the
  widest (0.19 mm): the ramp start meets the stock top. They are a load question for the entry
  gate, not a pass-width question.
- **P22c (capped bound)**: on idx 1 at this HEAD no D65 step is over the ceiling plus both cells
  except 12 samples on short 2D caps (max 17.76 mm at ap 3.57). P22c is still correct as a rule,
  but its predicted effect on idx 1 ("no fed step over 8.8 mm") depends on the perimeter fix
  first.
- **P22e (corridor trochoid)**: no case on idx 1. No wide sample points to a "no field" piece.
  Per the Q3 ruling, P22e can wait.
- **The uncapped draped step** (50 samples, ap up to 6.4, F 8475): the stock-read split read the
  step at or under `c` on the planner stock, and the sim reads it near a full slot. This is a
  planner-sim gap of `step_load` (cell 1.5 mm on an 18 mm tool), worth one look in P22c.
