## Goal

and a row without one does not compile. No finding refuses generation or export on any surface.

export, load exceeded) and the two load refusals beside them (unmodeled, stale simulation) become
warnings with ids. The export result and the G-code header name each warning. The GUI may ask one
confirm click for a `Danger::Machine` warning; MCP and the CLI never wait or refuse.
