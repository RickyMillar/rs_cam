### E1: the generators that cannot compute with a wrong tool kind

VCarve, Inlay, Inlay Plug and Chamfer read the V-bit half angle
(`curve_engrave.rs` `vbit_half_angle`: `included_angle / 2`). Only a V-bit carries a checked
`included_angle` (`ToolConfig` domain check, `tool_config.rs`), so these four stay NotReady with
the tool-kind reason. Bore, Pencil, Scallop, Unified Finish and Spiral Finish take any
`MillingCutter`; for them the tool kind warns. The buried face warns on every op (the generator
computes with it).
