## 1. Predictions (written before the code)

### 1.1 How this note reads the open points

- **Q6.** Under `ConstantChip` the calculator does not read the SFM RPM. A row with a printed RPM
  starts at the chart RPM and rises. A row with no printed RPM takes the minimum of the named
  limits when the ceiling is set, and refuses (`RowPrintsNoRpm`) when the ceiling is empty. Under
  `MatchChart` the SFM RPM stays: no rule replaces it there. The eight
  `base_cutting_speed_m_min` rows and `FALLBACK_RPM` stay in F201; the count 142 of the brief
  agrees with this reading.
- **The rule only rises** (design section 2). When the minimum of the limits is at or below the
  chart RPM, Step 5c keeps the chart RPM. Steps 6, 7 and 9c then act as today. An operator ceiling
  below the chart RPM does not lower the RPM.
- **A raw `calculate` with a gap** (no validated door) keeps the `MatchChart` numbers and gives
  `rpm_choice: None`. The refusal is at the one door `validate_tool_for_operation`.

## 2. Findings where the design and the code disagree

1. **Q11: no Suggest door carries the op feed cap.** The design says "Step 7 reads
   `op_cutting_ceiling(feed_max_rate)`". `feed_max_rate` is a `DressupConfig` field. Every feeds
   door takes `(operation, tool, material, machine, lut, strategy)`: `feeds_input_for_operation`,
   `feeds_result_for_operation`, `feeds_preview_for_operation`, `SafeEnvelope::for_cut`, the GUI
   `FeedsInputs` (67 call sites of the three builders). What P21c does: `FeedsInput::feed_max_rate`
   is new, and Step 5c (the XY limit), Step 7 and the Step 9c envelope clamp read one function,
   `calculator/spindle.rs::op_ceiling` = `machine.op_cutting_ceiling(input.feed_max_rate)`. The
   product builder sets `None`, so the op cap does not reach Suggest yet. A partial wiring (only
   the doors that hold a `SuggestContext`) makes the card and the apply disagree, so P21c wires
   none. The shape of the rule does not change. The wiring of every door needs a lead ruling.
2. **`RpmLimit::Acceleration` is not a member.** Design section 2.7 lists it. Under A3 nothing
   constructs it, and the workspace lints refuse dead code. The report is
   `machine::kinematics::reached_feed_share` (`ReachedFeedShare`), the session door
   `ProjectSession::reached_feed_share_at`, and MCP `basis.rpm.reached_feed_share`. No warning
   threshold exists: a threshold is a new number with no source. P21d adds the member with the cap.
3. **The power limit of a cut that is over the power at the chart RPM.** Design section 2.5 says
   the power "cannot set n" on a constant-torque drive below its rated RPM. The code admits an RPM
   when the ratio `P_req / P_avail` is at or below 1, or at or below its value at the chart RPM.
   So a cut with R > 1 still rises (the ratio holds), and the Step 6 ladder lowers the depth or
   the width as at the chart RPM. A limit that admits the spindle maximum is infinite in
   `RpmChoice::limits` (JSON `null`).
4. **The add door ignores the project policy.** `session/op_draft.rs::suggest_for` calls Suggest
   with `SpindleStrategy::default()`. A new op thus seeds at the chart RPM under a `constant_chip`
   project. Not changed (outside the P21c scope); `seed_feeds` and the apply read the policy.
5. **Q17 (the envelope chip "RPM varies with ae") is not built.** Section 9 lists it as a risk
   guard, not in the P21c scope of section 7.

### 2.1 Open item (lead ruling 2026-11-10): the op feed cap does not reach Suggest

Ruling on finding 1: keep `FeedsInput::feed_max_rate` with the product builder at `None`, and one
`op_cutting_ceiling(feed_max_rate)` for Steps 5c, 7 and 9c. P21c does not wire the doors. The
wiring is its own change, only if an acceptance row needs it. It touches the 67 call sites of
`feeds_input_for_operation`, `feeds_result_for_operation` and `feeds_explain_for_operation`
(20 in product code, 47 in tests), plus `feeds_preview_for_operation`, `SafeEnvelope::for_cut`
and the GUI `FeedsInputs`.

What this leaves unchanged:

- With `None`, `op_cutting_ceiling(None)` is `cutting_ceiling().at_least(1.0)`. On every profile
  with a ceiling of 1 mm/min or more that is the value of `cutting_feed_ceiling_mm_min()`, which
  Step 7 read before. So each Suggest feed and RPM under `MatchChart` is the same as before P21c,
  and the constant-chip XY limit reads the machine ceiling (wanaka: 15000, section 6.1 of the
  design: `n_xy` = 15000 / chip per rev).
- An op with a `feed_max_rate` below its Suggest feed still gets a Suggest feed and RPM above the
  cap, as before P21c. The cap still acts after generation: the modulator, the gates and the
  power figure read `op_steady_feed` (P20e), and the Feed row shows the "cap N" chip.
- No wanaka acceptance row grades the op feed cap, so no row of `wanaka_21.md` depends on it.

### 2.2 Lead rulings on the findings (2026-11-10, applied before the final gates)

- Finding 3 and section 1.1 "the rule only rises", changed: the operator ceiling, the spindle
  maximum and the printed vendor `rpm_max` ALWAYS bind. A chart RPM above one of them comes down
  to it, and the feed follows to hold the chip. The cutting ceiling, the Z rate, the power and
  the chart RPM of an empty ceiling only stop the rise; the power ladder, Step 7 and Step 9c own
  a move below the chart RPM. `RpmChoice::binding` is the limit that sets the RPM or stops the
  rise (`calculator/spindle.rs::always_binds_limit`).
- Finding 4, in scope: the add door `op_draft.rs::suggest_for` reads
  `PostConfig::spindle_policy()`. On a `ConstantChipUnstated` refusal it adds the op with the
  defaults and no feeds, and `ToolpathDraft::feeds_refusal` holds the text, as for `Unbacked`
  (a Suggest refusal refuses no op). `ToolChoice::FirstFitting` and `tool_fit` read the same call.
- Q6 (SFM stays under `MatchChart`), finding 2 (no `Acceleration` member, no threshold) and
  finding 1 (section 2.1) stand. Finding 5 is not in scope.
- Sentry arms added: a ceiling under the chart RPM gives the ceiling
  (`constant_chip_rises..`, arm f); the add door (`constant_chip_refuses..`, arm f).

  `MILLING_CEILING_LARGE_RPM`, `milling_rpm_ceiling_for_diameter`, `milling_rpm_ceiling_row`,
  `step2b_max_speed`, `step2b_milling_tier`, `RpmLimit::Legacy*`.
  `SpindleScaleReason::MaxSpeedPolicy` is `ConstantChip`.
- `MachineProfile::states_axis_rates`, `states_axis_accelerations`, `with_axis_limits`;
  `CuttingCeilingSource::label`.
- Surfaces: the Spindle row chip (binding limit, hover lists every limit), the `why.rs` sentence,

  and one table line. The sentence "Suggest does not read it yet" is gone from the GUI hover, the
