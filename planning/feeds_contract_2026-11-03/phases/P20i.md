HEAD `5888b1344`. Agent phase; no commit. Defects from `P20g_wanaka.md` 4.1 and 4.2.

`envelope_placement` already computed `MeasuredPoint::power_kw` at the effective feed with the

sample against the drawn depth curve of the Power limit.

## 2. Cause

### 2.1 Defect 1: the finding judges power at the stated op feed

`SafeEnvelope::place` judges a measured point against `Edge::ApCurve` of `LimitKind::Power`.
`power_limit` draws that curve once, before the simulation, at the operator's stated feed and
RPM (`operator_fits_power`). The emitted per-move feed (P20g: move 1697 at 2360) never reaches
the curve. Thus the count (5076 of 30557) cannot move when the modulator lowers a feed.

### 2.2 Defect 2: the grid prints the planner-cap case at the nominal width

The grid power is `FeedsResult::power_kw`, the maximum over the op's `PowerCases` (K7 change 3).
Wanaka idx 1 ("Back bulk rough 18 mm", 3D Rough) has `trochoid_cap_mult = 1.6`, so the
`PlannerCap` case evaluates the width `min(1.6 ae, D)`. The grid row is labelled with the
nominal `ae`. A fit that takes the label as the width inflates both terms:

| Term | Fit / acceptance | Prediction of the planner-cap case |
|---|---|---|
| `Ks` (shear term, linear in `ae`) | 93.6 / 58.516 = 1.5996 | 1.6 |
| `F_edge` (edge term, linear in `psi`) | 5.91 / 4.595 = 1.2862 | `acos(1 - 2 x 1.6 x 2.589 / 18) / acos(1 - 2 x 2.589 / 18)` = 1.2863 |

Both ratios agree to 4 digits. No second law, no RPM change and no derate are in the grid: it is
the same law as Suggest, at the planner-cap width. The case has a source (the op's own
`trochoid_cap_mult`, K7 change 3), so it stays. It is not named on the grid point: that is the
defect. Sentry (b) measures the same engagement through each door.

## 3. Design

1. ONE power judgement for a measured sample: the gate's. `envelope_placement` takes the
   machine. For each sample that the power gate grades (cutting, not air, an arc, steady state)
   it hands `SafeEnvelope::placement_of_measured` the gate's figure: `sample_power_kw` at
   `effective_feed_for_sample`, against `machine.power_at_rpm(sample rpm)`. The new door is
   `tool_load::power::gate_sample_power`; `power::evaluate` calls it, so both read one function.
2. `place` skips the drawn Power edge for a measured point. It judges power by the figure:
   margin `(available - required) / available`. A sample with no figure (no force line, a sample
   the gate does not grade) is not judged for power, as the gate does not judge it.
3. `JudgedValue` states its unit (`measured`, `limit`, `unit`: "mm" or "kW"), so the finding
   prints "power 1.42 kW against the limit 1.13 kW at F 2360".
4. `SpeedsPoint` names its power case and width (`power_case`, `power_width_mm`). The grid law
   does not change.

## 7. Decisions and open items

- D1: the planner-cap and slot cases stay in the pre-sim grid and the drawn edge (source: the
  op's `CutSpec` and `trochoid_cap_mult`, K7 change 3). The grid names them; a measured sample
  does not use them.
- D2: a measured sample's power is judged on every op whose gate grades it, also on an op whose
  depth is fixed (the drawn edge does not place such an op). The gate grades those samples too.
- D3: `JudgedValue` fields are now `measured`, `limit`, `unit`, `feed_mm_min` (lineage JSON and
  the MCP `get_toolpath_lineage` payload change their names).
- Open 1: the envelope placement reads the stock material only; the gate splits populations by
  material (S6). A stock change "cut as" its own material can still differ.
- Open 2: the wanaka acceptance (idx 1 count, the other 7 rows) needs a GUI run from a binary
  built after this change.
