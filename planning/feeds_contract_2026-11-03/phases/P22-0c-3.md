## 3. Item 2: the wanaka move 21244

The static check is wrong on that move; the kernel is right. Mechanism, from
the code path (`emit_ramp` terrain re-plan returns `None`, so the plunge
fallback runs `plunge_refusal(safety, ramp_start_z, end)`):

1. The static depth starts at the entry start height `ramp_start_z` =
   material top + `entry_clearance_mm`. The 0.5 mm of air counts as wood:
   1.79 mm = 1.29 mm + 0.5 mm.
2. The material top is the planner's conservative top over the whole entry
   disc. Only wood within the core radius meets the core; the ring cuts the
   rest. The kernel read the live stock under the core: no wood above the
   1.00 mm protrusion.

Reproduced on a fixture (`--lib dressup::tests::the_static_plunge_and_the_kernel_disagree_where_the_static_check_is_wrong_p22_0c`):
0.7 mm of wood gives a static 1.2 mm plunge and no kernel contact; a disc at
5 mm with the core column cut to 0.7 mm gives a static plunge and no kernel
contact. Not re-measured on wanaka 21 (no GUI run).
