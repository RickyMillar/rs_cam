Scope from the lead brief (rows idx 1 feed and idx 14 feed of the wanaka table; operator ruling
Q2, `P22_DESIGN.md` section 9). Base `31b23df5b`. No commit, no bless. Section 1 to 3 were written before the code; the lead ruled on section 3 (section 4); the code
and the results follow (sections 5 and 6).

## 1. Survey

### 1.1 Where the lift and the "planner cap" case come from

- The lift: `feeds/calculator/feed.rs::step5_feed`,
  `thinning.lift(cut.ae, effective_d.mm())`. `cut.ae` is the nominal width: the op's stated
  stepover (`operation_feeds_hints`), else the family default. One rule,
  `geometry::RadialThinning::lift`: `1 / sqrt(1 - (1 - 2 ae/D)^2)` below half the diameter, else 1.
- The power case: `FeedsInput::power_cases` = `PowerCases::of_operation(operation)` =
  `of_cut(cut_spec, operation.planner_engagement_cap_mult())`. `PowerCases::widths` gives
  `(PlannerCap, min(mult x ae, D))`. On wanaka idx 1: 1.6 x 5.5 = 8.8 mm
  (`shipped_power.case_ae_mm`).
- Other readers of `RadialThinning::lift` do not read the nominal Suggest width and need no
  change: the gate display (`tool_load/display.rs`), the modulator
  (`dressup/feed_modulation.rs:1116`) and `dressup/feedopt.rs` read the width of each move;
  `geometry::UnliftedFeed` (`geometry.rs:129`), `suggest/adaptive_entry.rs:151`,
  `efficiency.rs:209`, `toolpath_diagnostics.rs:50` and the power ladder (`power.rs:363`) divide
  or re-apply the lift at the same width as Step 5. If Step 5 reads another width, each of these
  five must read the same one, or the plunge, the ramp and the efficiency figure drift.

### 1.4 idx 6 (survey only)

idx 6 is a contour Pocket, 2 mm tool, stepover 0.8. Its lift comes from the same Step 5 line at
the nominal 0.8 mm: `1 / sqrt(1 - 0.2^2)` = 1.0206 (+2 %). The op's `CutSpec` already says
`FirstPass::Slot`, and the power set already has the `Slot` case at D, but the feed lift does not
read the cases. It is the same code path as item 1 only under one general rule: "the lift reads
the widest width that the plan cuts at the full feed" (the maximum of `PowerCases::widths`).
That rule gives lift 1 to every `FirstPass::Slot` op with a stepover (Pocket, Zigzag, and a 2D
Adaptive with slot lines): the whole pocket then runs at the slot feed. The smaller correct
change is outside Suggest: the generator or a dressup feeds the first ring at the unlifted feed
(`geometry::UnliftedFeed`) and the later rings at the lifted feed. Not coded.

## 3. Open design points (the reason for the stop)

1. **`Adaptive3d` under `ContourParallel`.** Brief item 1 says "for ops that do not hold the pass
   ceiling, change nothing". Brief item 2 says Suggest stops reading `trochoid_cap_mult`. This op
   does not hold the ceiling and is the only place where that read has an effect that item 1 does
   not replace. Options: (a) it loses the `PlannerCap` case (power at the nominal width); FM1
   builds its 40 `Adaptive3d` cells on the default strategy, so their power column moves; (b) it
   keeps the 1.6 read until a contour-parallel width rule exists (item 2 then holds only for the
   geodesic arm).
2. **2D `Adaptive`.** By the code it holds the ceiling, so by item 1 its lift and a new
   `PlannerCap` case read `c D`. That moves the feed of the 40 FM1 `Adaptive` cells (down, by the
   ratio of the two lifts) and the 2D rows of the wanaka table if any. The brief names only the
   geodesic arm. Include it, or leave it for its own phase?
3. **The five secondary readers of section 1.1** must read the same width as Step 5. The clean
   door is one value on the input, for example `FeedsInput::full_feed_width` built beside
   `power_cases`, read by `step5_feed`, `UnliftedFeed::at_cut`, the ladder, the adaptive-entry
   pass and the efficiency figure. I ask for a go on that shape before I touch five files.
4. **The power ladder's width rung** lowers `ae` and re-lifts at the new width. With the lift at
   `c D`, the rung must recompute `c D` from the new width (the ceiling is a function of the
   stepover). That is the same function; I state it so the review expects it.

## 4. Lead rulings (2026-11-10)

- Point 3: go. One value, the full-feed width, built beside `power_cases` from the planner
  function; all six readers read it (Step 5 lift, `UnliftedFeed::at_cut`, the power ladder width
  rung, `adaptive_entry.rs`, `efficiency.rs`, `toolpath_diagnostics.rs`). The rule in words: the
  chip-thinning lift reads the widest width the planner holds at the full feed; for an op with no
  such planner bound it is the nominal stepover, as today.
- Point 2: include 2D `Adaptive`. It holds the ceiling by its invariant, and one rule must not
  have two answers. The 40 FM1 `Adaptive` cells move; reason "the lift reads the pass ceiling
  width the planner holds". No bless until the lead rules on the numbers.
- Point 1: (b). `Adaptive3d` + `ContourParallel` keeps the 1.6 planner-cap read for now: that
  plan holds no bound, and dropping the case removes a power check. The catalog function returns
  the multiplier only for `ContourParallel`; the geodesic arm no longer reaches it.
- idx 6: not in Suggest.

## 5.1 The shape that landed

- `compute::catalog::PlannerWidth` (`None`, `PassCeiling`, `CapMult`) and
  `OperationConfig::planner_width()` replace `planner_engagement_cap_mult`.
  `PlannerWidth::pass_ceiling_mm` composes the planner's two public functions,
  `adaptive::pass_engagement_limit` and `radial_woc_fraction_from_leading_arc`, as
  `adaptive3d/clearing.rs` does. `PassLoad::swept_width` itself is private to `adaptive/`, which
  P23a must not edit.
- The value sits inside `feeds::engagement::PowerCases` (not a new `FeedsInput` field): the set
  already rides on the input, and the power case and the lift then cannot read two widths.
  `PowerCases::full_feed_width(ae, D)` is the one door of the six readers; `widths` gives the
  `PlannerCap` case the same width.

### 6.1 The FM1 move (reason: the lift reads the pass ceiling width the planner holds)

TaperedBallNose 8). Sim subset: 24 differences on 8 `Adaptive` cells.

| `force_n` | 19 | -2.35 % to -1.42 % |

Why the RPM rises and the feed holds (the prediction said "feed down"): these cells sit on the
machine feed ceiling (4000 mm/min), where Step 7 arm L sets the RPM to `ceiling / chip per rev`.
The smaller lift gives a smaller chip per rev, so the same capped feed needs a higher RPM. The
plunge and the ramp follow the RPM. A cell under the ceiling moves in feed, as wanaka idx 1 does
(9332 -> 9123). 10 of the 40 `Adaptive` cells do not move; I did not classify them (a refused cell and a
lift of 1 at both widths are the two cases the rule allows).
