What the model needs to compute a stepover and a depth

Feeds contract P13 step 0, 2026-11-05. Code at 36fd50c54. Every number on this page has a claim ID; assert_claims.py checks each one. Section G comes first at the operator's request: the assumptions, then the math.

Short answer. The model cannot compute a Pocket stepover: no limit it holds falls with the width. The peak force is almost flat in width (15.33 N at 0.35 D, 15.92 N at D) C09, the Pocket power is judged at a full slot C04, and the removal-rate optimum is the full width in every cell and sweep C11 C12. A frame spring explains a shallow depth, not a narrow width C10. The factors that can pick a narrow width (chip evacuation, finish, chatter, tool life, corner geometry) are all ignored today.

B. Frame compliance

No machine, belt, screw or collet stiffness is printed in the repo C17. It would enter as a spring in series with the tool beam: δ = Fpeak (1/kframe + ctool(ap)) ≤ target. The target is 200 µm today, F201 Unsourced; a user-stated finish allowance is a better target.

Measurement protocol

  1. Put a dial indicator (0.01 mm or finer) on a magnetic base on the bed, not the gantry.
  2. Clamp a short steel pin of the largest diameter the collet takes. Compute its compliance with the shipped beam and subtract it.
  3. Power the motors, as in a cut.
  4. X and Y: pull the pin along the axis with a spring scale or a weight over a pulley. Indicate on the far side, on the same line.
  5. Z: pull up and down at the collet nut; indicate on the nut face.
  6. Use 6 loads from 0 to about 120 N (section A peaks are 14-104 N). Load up, then unload. Do 3 runs per axis.
  7. Repeat at the gantry centre and both ends of Y, with Z high and low.
  8. Fit deflection against force on the loading branch above the first step. Slope = compliance (mm/N) minus the pin; k = 1/slope (N/mm). Report the intercept as backlash. Use the worst position.

C. Chatter

A stability-lobe model needs the tool-tip frequency response (natural frequency, damping, modal stiffness per direction) and the radial coefficient Kr, which the code sets to 0. The repo prints neither. A phone-microphone tap gives the frequency, the ring-down an estimate of damping, and the B protocol the static stiffness; a real FRF needs a force hammer, per tool and stickout. Recommendation: out of scope for P13; state "the cut is stable" on the card. Revisit after B.

D. Chip evacuation

No printed numeric rule for a wood or plastic slot from chip evacuation or heat. The repo has Sorotec's slot depth "bis 2-facher Durchmesser" with no reason, Harvey's miniature-tool step-down rule (not wood), and qualitative upcut advice (Onsrud, Vortex). It stays a stated assumption: "chips always clear".

E. The removal-rate aim

MRR = ae · ap · vf, vf = min(rpm Z fz lift(ae), cap). Below the cap MRR grows as ae/sin ψ; at the cap as ae. So the objective prefers the full width unless a limit falls steeply with the width, and none does C11 C12 C13.
