[chapter page 3, section 5.1 Sizing and grooving, "Operation notes"]
Operation notes                   Recommended plunging methods:
The following plunging methods are recommended for sizing and grooving tools:
Ramp plunging                                 Spiral plunging
Router bits with mainly negative cutting shear angles and HW solid router bits with
RH/LD and LH/RD and router bits without plunging edge are not suitable for axial
plunging.
Axial plunging

[chapter page 4, tools with alternating twist (compression)]
Tools with alternating twist should
plunge at least 0.5 mm deeper into the
material than the specified lpos.
ap min = lpos. + 0.5 mm

[insert cutter, text lines 808-810]
Tungsten carbide turnblade knife clamped by wedge. Design without plunging tip
only suitable for ramp plunging. Design with plunging tip limited suitable for axial
plunging.

[DP (diamond) shank cutter, text lines 3186-3190]
Technical information:
Solid tungsten carbide tool body for increased stability and smooth running. DP face
edge suitable for plunging. Slightly positive shear angle for improved chip removal
when ramp plunging. Axial infeed for grooving and sizing maximum 1.0 - 1.5 x D.
Resharpenable up to 3 times with normal wear.
