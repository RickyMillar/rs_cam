--- post 2, user Tod1d, staff flag False, 2023-08-06
For 1/4" cutters and up, I use 3° for aluminum.  Shallower for smaller cutters. 5° for wood.
For wood, yes it does have it’s place. Anywhere a plunge will result in burning the wood. I use ramps on my surfacing paths.
