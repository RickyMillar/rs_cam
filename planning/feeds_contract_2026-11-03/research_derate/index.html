1. Answer

  • The vendor-backed feed is rpm x chip x Z x the printed depth tier (feeds/mod.rs:2679). The long-tool share and chip thinning are reported, not applied [E01]. The machine enters only as limits: rpm range, power curve, feed cap. Limits take the minimum; they do not multiply.
  • The concern holds for geometry. Light-machine margins sit in four places: the Shapeoko table, machine-blind typed constants, RigidityProfile (itself a Shapeoko port [B08]), and the LUT filter [F03].
  • One true product of two Shapeoko ports exists: the formula chip times the formula surface speed, on the 60 FM1 cells with no vendor chip [E05].
  • Stored lineage is one stamp per field. The in-memory tree holds no numbers, and no surface prints it for the speeds [G06] [G07].

2. The table and what reaches it

operation_default_profile (feeds/mod.rs:3513) holds 21 rows, 42 factor cells. Source: "DOC/WOC matrix of reference/shapeoko_feeds_and_speeds calcs.rs (not in the repo)" [A01]. The 25 registry rows reach 8 (family, role) pairs; no op uses the Face family (the Face op reads Pocket) [A02]. The calculator reads the table only on an axis the op does not state [A06]. Every reached ap factor except Adaptive Roughing is at or below 1.0, so the depth tier stays 1.0 [A05]. Live width values: Pocket R 0.35, Trace F 0.02, Parallel F 0.03, Scallop F 0.025, Contour F 0.05 [A04].

5. Proposal

  • One place for the machine margin: RigidityProfile. The base is machine-neutral: vendor rows and the P11 model.
  • Delete the table ap column and the Face rows; depth_cap_mm is the one adaptive depth (M4).
  • Pocket width reads woc_roughing_factor; adaptive width reads adaptive_woc_factor; delete the ADAPTIVE_* width rows (M5, M6). L8.
  • Move the slot cap into RigidityProfile (M2). L8.
  • State doc_roughing_factor as a fraction of the vendor depth; the Shapeoko source is the Carbide 3D chart. The vendor 1/2/3 x D table becomes a visible limit. L6.
  • Move the formula chip off the machine; replace SFM, rpm tiers and typed copies by sourced rules or reads of the one place. L8.
